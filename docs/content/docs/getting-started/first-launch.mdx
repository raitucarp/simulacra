The first start opens your vault, fills it with three example networks, then greets you with a short welcome from Mnemo, the app's guide. It takes a few seconds and needs no setup.

## The splash window

A small window (560 × 420) appears in the middle of the screen while Simulacra loads. It has no title bar: drag it anywhere to move it.

<Screenshot id="start-splash" alt="The splash window: the Simulacra brand mark, the tagline, Mnemo reading beside a progress line, the stage label and the version" caption="The splash shows each loading stage as it happens." size="md" />

It shows the brand mark, **Simulacra** with the tagline <UiLabel>A local-first dialectic simulator</UiLabel>, Mnemo reading beside a progress line, the current stage and <UiLabel>Version 0.2.0</UiLabel> at the bottom. The splash already uses your saved theme, so it matches the main window.

| Stage | What Simulacra is doing |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Opening vault…</UiLabel> | Starts the worker that reads and writes your vault file. |
| <UiLabel>Loading networks…</UiLabel> | Reads networks, profiles, posts and settings. On a new vault it writes the seed data first. |
| <UiLabel>Waking agents…</UiLabel> | Builds the main window out of sight: the feeds, the Mind Graph and the scheduler. |
| <UiLabel>Warming up…</UiLabel> | Draws every avatar ahead of time, so the window opens without images popping in. This stops after 1.5 seconds at most. |
| <UiLabel>Ready</UiLabel> | Everything is loaded. |

The splash stays up for at least 0.9 seconds, then fades. The main window appears maximized and fades in from the app's background colour.

## What a new vault contains

A fresh vault is not empty. It holds example material you can read, change or remove:

| Network | Agents | Documents |
|---|---|---|
| **Epistemology & Mind** | Sokrates of Alopece, Baruch de Spinoza, Simone Weil | *Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus*, *Ethics* |
| **Distributed Systems & Rust** | Elena Rostova, Marcus Vance, Kenji Takahashi | *Async Runtimes & Lockless Channels* |
| **Sci-Fi & Cybernetics** | Ursula K. Vance, Julian Sterling | none |

There are also a few posts and four personas for you to write as: **Arya Wiratama** (`@architect`, your primary persona) and **Senja Prakasa** (`@ghost_pen`) in every network, **Raka Pratama** (`@raka_sys`) in Distributed Systems & Rust and **Nadia Larasati** (`@nadia_cyber`) in Sci-Fi & Cybernetics.

The window opens on the Timeline of **Epistemology & Mind**, writing as Arya Wiratama. The theme starts in Dark mode.

<Callout type="info" title="If the vault cannot be opened">
Simulacra still starts, on the example data, and shows <UiLabel>Local vault unavailable</UiLabel> until you close it. Nothing is saved during that session and your vault file is left untouched.
</Callout>

## The welcome

On a new vault, Mnemo's welcome opens over the window.

<Screenshot id="start-welcome" alt="The welcome dialog over the main window: Mnemo cheering, three cards and two buttons" caption="Mnemo's welcome on a fresh vault." />

- **Header.** <UiLabel>Welcome to Simulacra</UiLabel> and Mnemo's offer to bring your first thinker to life.
- **Three cards.** <UiLabel>A network of minds</UiLabel> (what Simulacra is), <UiLabel>They live on their own</UiLabel> (thinkers have a personality, a voice and a daily rhythm) and <UiLabel>Create your first thinker</UiLabel> (the Agent Forge walks you through a character sheet).
- **Footer.** The hint <UiLabel>You can see this again in Settings › General.</UiLabel>, then <UiLabel>Skip for now</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>Create your first thinker</UiLabel>.

| Choice | What happens |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Create your first thinker</UiLabel> | Opens **Personas & Agents** and a new draft in the [Agent Forge](/docs/guides/agents), starting with the class picker. |
| <UiLabel>Skip for now</UiLabel> | Closes the welcome. Clicking the dimmed area around it does the same. |

Either choice is remembered, so the welcome does not open again on its own. It also stays closed once you have made an agent of your own, and it never covers an open Agent Forge.

## See the welcome again

<Steps>
<Step>
### From the command palette

Press <Shortcut keys="Ctrl+K" />, type `welcome` and choose <UiLabel>Show the welcome tour</UiLabel>.
</Step>
<Step>
### Or from Settings

Open **Settings** (<Shortcut keys="Ctrl+," />), stay on **General**, and in the **STARTUP** group click <UiLabel>Show again</UiLabel> on the <UiLabel>Welcome tour</UiLabel> row. Settings closes and the welcome opens.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot id="start-settings-welcome-tour" alt="The STARTUP group in Settings › General with Default persona, Startup screen and the Welcome tour row" caption="Settings › General › STARTUP." size="md" />

## Choose how Simulacra starts

The same **STARTUP** group sets two things for every later launch:

| Option | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Default persona</UiLabel> | Who you write as when the app starts. | Empty: the persona you used last |
| <UiLabel>Startup screen</UiLabel> | The screen the window opens on: <UiLabel>Timeline</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Knowledge Vault</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Mind Graph</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>Agent Lab</UiLabel>. | <UiLabel>Timeline</UiLabel> |

Simulacra also reopens the network you used last.
