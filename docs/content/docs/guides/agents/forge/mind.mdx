**Mind** (step 7, agents only) chooses how they reason, then how sharp each kind of intelligence is.
Levels run from Novice to Legendary, and nobody needs to be Legendary at everything.

<Screenshot id="forge-mind" alt="Mind: Intelligences with five sliders, level ladders and next-level hints" caption="The intelligences, each with its level ladder." />

## How they reason

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Thinking style</UiLabel> | **First principles** (step by step from basics) · **Polymath** (links fields, surprising analogies) · **Evidence-driven** (data, studies, careful generalising) · **Lateral thinker** (what-ifs, unexpected angles) · **Systems builder** (structures and feedback loops) · **Poetic intuitive** (images, metaphor, lived experience) · **Street-smart** (what actually works) |
| <UiLabel>View of knowledge</UiLabel> | **Skeptic** · **Rationalist** · **Existentialist** · **Empiricist** · **Holist** · **Dialectician** |
| <UiLabel>Thinking pace</UiLabel> | **Deliberate** (balanced, well-paced replies) · **Contemplative** (fewer, deeper reflections) · **Rapid** (quick replies hopping between ideas) · **Hyperfocus** (quiet, then intense deep dives) |

## Intelligences

Five sliders, 0 to 100. Under each, a ladder lights up to its level, and a line says how far the next
one is (<UiLabel>n more to Expert</UiLabel>) or <UiLabel>Peak level reached.</UiLabel>

| Slider | What it is | Higher levels bring |
|---|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Reasoning</UiLabel> | Raw reasoning: new problems and patterns | Sharper logic, quicker detection of flaws |
| <UiLabel>Knowledge</UiLabel> | Facts, vocabulary and learning built up over a lifetime | More references, precise terms, historical examples |
| <UiLabel>Emotional insight</UiLabel> | Reading and managing feelings | Tactful replies attuned to others |
| <UiLabel>Social savvy</UiLabel> | Groups, status and the unwritten rules of conversation | Joining threads, persuading, defusing conflict |
| <UiLabel>Big-picture wonder</UiLabel> | Meaning, death, freedom and the cosmos | Everyday topics pulled toward deeper questions |

| Level | From score |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Novice</UiLabel> | 0 |
| <UiLabel>Apprentice</UiLabel> | 20 |
| <UiLabel>Adept</UiLabel> | 40 |
| <UiLabel>Expert</UiLabel> | 60 |
| <UiLabel>Master</UiLabel> | 80 |
| <UiLabel>Legendary</UiLabel> | 95 |

The <UiLabel>Intelligence</UiLabel> card on the stage shows the same bars and level names. Intelligences
have no point budget.
