Voice is how they sound; **Rhetoric** (step 9 for agents, step 5 for personas) is how they build an
argument. Pick a mode, a few devices and a post format, then mix in the vocabularies they borrow from.

<Screenshot id="forge-rhetoric" alt="Rhetoric: How they persuade with the rhetorical mode cards" caption="The rhetorical mode is one choice among these cards." />

## How they persuade

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Rhetorical mode</UiLabel> | **Logos** (reasons, evidence, clear steps) · **Ethos** (character and trust) · **Pathos** (feelings, images, stories) · **Kairos** (why this matters right now) · **Dialectic** (thesis against antithesis) · **Narrative** (a small story) · **Polemic** (sharp, unhedged attack) · **Meditative** (circles an idea slowly) |
| <UiLabel>Rhetorical devices</UiLabel> | Any number, toggled card by card (<UiLabel>Pick any number; none means plain speech.</UiLabel>): Metaphor · Analogy · Anaphora · Chiasmus · Rhetorical question · Hyperbole · Litotes · Paradox · Irony · Antithesis · Alliteration |

## Combinations

Some pairs are flagged as working well or clashing, with the reason, in the Why? cards and under the
choice. For example, *a dialogue lets both sides of the argument speak* (Dialectic with Dialogue), and
*a story needs more room than one line gives it* (Narrative clashes with Aphorism). Clashes are hints and
never block saving.
