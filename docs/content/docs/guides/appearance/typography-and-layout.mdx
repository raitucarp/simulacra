**TYPOGRAPHY & LAYOUT** in Settings › Appearance scales the whole interface. Each choice is a row like the other settings rows, its name on the left and its chips on the right, and applies at once, on top of whichever theme is active.

## Options

| Row | Options | Default | What it changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Font family</UiLabel> | Segoe UI, Calibri, Georgia, Verdana, Trebuchet MS | Segoe UI | The font of the interface, headings and post text. Code keeps its monospace font. |
| <UiLabel>Text size</UiLabel> | <UiLabel>Small</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Default</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Large</UiLabel> | Default | The size of all interface text: Small is 92%, Large 110% of Default. |
| <UiLabel>Density</UiLabel> | <UiLabel>Compact</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Comfortable</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Cozy</UiLabel> | Comfortable | How much space surrounds every element: Compact is 75%, Cozy 125% of Comfortable. |
| <UiLabel>Corners</UiLabel> | <UiLabel>Sharp</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Default</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Round</UiLabel> | Default | How rounded corners are: Sharp is 35%, Round 160% of Default. Pills stay fully round. |

The five font families are those installed with Windows.

**Density** and **Font size** can also be found by searching Settings (`compact`, `zoom`): the results show the current value with <UiLabel>Show in Appearance</UiLabel>, and their reset arrow puts the default back.

## Panel layout

The last row of the card is <UiLabel>Panel layout</UiLabel>: <UiLabel>Drag any divider to resize a panel; double-click it to restore that split.</UiLabel>

Every resizable split in the app, such as the Settings rail, remembers the size you drag it to. <UiLabel>Reset layout</UiLabel> puts every split back to its default size and forgets the saved sizes.

## Make the text larger

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open Appearance

Click the palette icon in the sidebar footer.
</Step>
<Step>
### Pick Large

In **TYPOGRAPHY & LAYOUT**, click <UiLabel>Large</UiLabel> beside <UiLabel>Text size</UiLabel>.
</Step>
<Step>
### Give it room (optional)

Click <UiLabel>Cozy</UiLabel> beside <UiLabel>Density</UiLabel> for more space around each element.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Tips and limits

- These choices belong to the theme settings, not the preferences: <UiLabel>Reset all settings</UiLabel> leaves them as they are. Reset them one by one, or with the arrows in Settings search.
- Reading width and reduced motion are in [READING COMFORT](/docs/guides/appearance#reading-comfort).
