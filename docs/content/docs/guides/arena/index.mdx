The Arena puts your agents to work on a question. In a **debate**, two to four agents argue a motion in a set format while a judge scores them and the transcript is mapped into claims that stand or fall. In the **thought lab**, every agent you choose answers each combination of a scenario's variables, so you can see where they disagree and what changes their minds.

## Open it

- Click <UiLabel>Arena</UiLabel> in the **left sidebar**.
- Press <Shortcut keys="Ctrl+9" />.
- Run a palette command:

| Command | Opens |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Arena</UiLabel> | The page as you left it |
| <UiLabel>New debate</UiLabel> | The Debate tab with its setup form |
| <UiLabel>New thought experiment</UiLabel> | The Thought lab tab |
| <UiLabel>Debate this thought</UiLabel> | The setup form with the thought open in the inspector as the motion |
| <UiLabel>Run a thought experiment on this thought</UiLabel> | The lab form with that thought as the premise |

From a post, open its <UiLabel>…</UiLabel> menu › <UiLabel>Actions</UiLabel> and pick <UiLabel>Debate this</UiLabel> (under Challenge) or <UiLabel>Run a thought experiment on this</UiLabel> (under Explore). Both fill the Arena form the same way. See [Post tools](/docs/guides/post-tools).

## Anatomy

1. **The header**: the art, <UiLabel>Arena</UiLabel>, and "Let agents debate a motion or answer a thought experiment. Every request counts toward today's AI budget."
2. **The tabs**: <UiLabel>Debate</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>Thought lab</UiLabel>.
3. **A notice** when something stopped a run (on the tab whose run it was), or when no AI key is set: <UiLabel>No AI key is set, so agents cannot speak yet. Add one in Settings › AI & Models.</UiLabel>
4. **Two columns**: the form on one side, the open debate or experiment on the other. With nothing open, the other side shows a short note and the saved list beneath it; once a record is open, the saved list moves under the form. Below the wide layout the columns stack, with the open record on top so you see it without scrolling.

## Saved debates and experiments

<Screenshot id="arena-saved" alt="The Saved debates list with two rows: a motion, its time and how far it got" caption="Every debate and experiment is saved as it runs." size="sm" />

<UiLabel>Saved debates</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>Saved experiments</UiLabel> lists your records (beside the form while nothing is open, below it once a record is), newest first, with their time and progress ("5 of 8 turns", <UiLabel>Judged</UiLabel>, "6 of 8 answers"). Click one to show it. A stopped one resumes where it stopped. With none yet: <UiLabel>No debates yet.</UiLabel> / <UiLabel>No experiments yet.</UiLabel>

## What a run costs

Every turn, verdict and answer is a request to an AI model, charged to the shared daily budget of **Settings › AI & Models › Daily request budget**, the same budget the tutor, post actions and indexing use. Each agent speaks through its own AI engine, with its own persona and current beliefs as its instructions; a judge without a persona uses the global model.

| Run | Requests |
|---|---|
| A debate | One per turn still to speak, two for judging (the verdict, then the claim map), and one belief update per debater |
| Continuing a stopped debate | What is left of the above |
| A thought experiment | One per agent per case, then one belief update per agent |

With **Settings › AI & Models › Ask before spending** on, the Arena asks first:

<Screenshot id="arena-spend-confirm" alt="The Send this to the AI? dialog: This sends about 10 requests to your AI provider, the requests left today, Don't ask again this session, and Send" caption="The confirm states every request the run still needs." size="sm" />

The dialog says how many requests the run sends and how many are left in today's budget. <UiLabel>Send</UiLabel> starts it; <UiLabel>Don't ask again this session</UiLabel> stops the question for the rest of the session.

Only one Arena run goes at a time. A stopped or failed run keeps everything said so far.

## Privacy

Agents in the Arena see the motion, the transcript or the scenario, and their own persona and beliefs. Your journal, mood, focus sessions and capsules never reach a prompt.
