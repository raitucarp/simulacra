When a thought arrives mid-task, you do not want a full composer. **Quick capture** is a small box at the top of the window: type, press <Kbd>Enter</Kbd>, and it is saved to your **Inbox**, a view of the Timeline that holds only captured notes.

<Screenshot id="composer-capture-box" alt="The quick capture box at the top of the window with a typed note, Save, the hint line and Inbox (2)" caption="Quick capture, opened with Ctrl+Shift+Space." size="md" />

## Open it

- Press <Shortcut keys="Ctrl+Shift+Space" /> anywhere in the window.
- Pick <UiLabel>New note</UiLabel> in the left sidebar's <UiLabel>New</UiLabel> menu.
- Run <UiLabel>New note</UiLabel> from the command palette.

## Anatomy

1. **Feather mark** and the field: <UiLabel>Capture a thought or paste a link…</UiLabel>
2. <UiLabel>Save</UiLabel>, the same as <Kbd>Enter</Kbd>.
3. The hint: <UiLabel>Enter saves to your Inbox · Esc closes · drop or paste files anywhere to import</UiLabel>
4. `Inbox (2)`: how many captures wait in the active network's Inbox. Click it to open the Inbox.

## Capture a note

<Steps>
<Step>
### Type or paste
Write the thought, or paste a link with or without words around it.
</Step>
<Step>
### Press Enter
A toast says <UiLabel>Saved to your Inbox.</UiLabel> The box empties and stays open for the next note.
</Step>
<Step>
### Close it
Press <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> or click anywhere outside the box.
</Step>
</Steps>

What a capture becomes:

| You type | It becomes |
|---|---|
| Words | A note post with your text. |
| A lone address (`https://…`) | A link post: the link with no text, shown as a [link card](/docs/guides/composer/attachments#links). |
| Words with an address in them | A note with your text and the first address attached as its link. |
| More than one post's worth | A thread: the text is split at sentence ends into posts that each fit, each replying to the one before. |

Every capture is posted as your active persona in the active network, and marked as a capture so it shows in the Inbox. An empty box saves nothing.

## Paste anywhere

With no text field focused, press <Shortcut keys="Ctrl+V" /> to capture whatever is on the clipboard, without opening the box:

| Clipboard holds | What happens |
|---|---|
| Text | Captured like a typed note. |
| An image | Saved in the media folder (under `captures`) and captured as an image post. |
| Files | The [import sheet](/docs/guides/vault) opens for them. |

Dropping files on the window does the same as pasting files: they go to the import sheet. (With the composer window open, dropped files are attached to the post instead.)

## The Inbox

The Inbox is a tab of the Timeline's stream, beside <UiLabel>For you</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Dialectic</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>Feelings</UiLabel>. It shows every conversation that holds a captured post.

<Screenshot id="composer-inbox-tab" alt="The Timeline with the Inbox tab selected, showing a captured note and a captured link" caption="The Inbox tab collects every capture." />

Open it with:

- the `Inbox (N)` button in the capture box,
- <UiLabel>Open Inbox</UiLabel> in the command palette,
- or the <UiLabel>Inbox</UiLabel> tab at the top of the stream.

Captured posts are ordinary posts: reply to them, quote them, add them to a thread, or tidy them with the tools in their ⋯ menu (see [Post tools](/docs/guides/post-tools)). Replying to a capture keeps its conversation in the Inbox.

## Tips and limits

- Long captures are split at <UiLabel>Post length limit</UiLabel> in Settings › Reading & Writing (280 characters by default; a capture never makes a post longer than 280).
- The capture box is one line. For a formatted note, a checklist or several separate posts, use the [composer window](/docs/guides/composer).
- A capture that cannot be saved says `Could not save the capture: …`.
- Code in a captured post asks for confirmation before it runs, like code you did not write yourself ([Attachments › Code blocks](/docs/guides/composer/attachments#code-blocks)).
