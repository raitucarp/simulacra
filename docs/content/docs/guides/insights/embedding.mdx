The Embedding map places every indexed post on a plane so that posts with similar meaning land near each other, whatever words they use. Clusters show the themes of a network; a lone point is a thought unlike the rest.

<Screenshot id="insights-embedding" alt="The Embedding map with coloured clusters of points, cluster labels and a tooltip listing nearest posts" caption="Posts by meaning, coloured by cluster, with one post's nearest neighbours." />

## Before you start

The map needs posts that have been embedded, which happens when semantic search is on and an embedding provider is configured in Settings › <UiLabel>Embeddings</UiLabel>. With fewer than two indexed posts in the period, the tab shows <UiLabel>No map of meaning yet</UiLabel>. See [Meaning search](/docs/concepts/meaning-search).

## Anatomy

1. **<UiLabel>Map of meaning</UiLabel>** card with <UiLabel>Fit to view</UiLabel> in its title row.
2. **<UiLabel>Colour by</UiLabel>** chips.
3. **The map.** One dot per post. Coloured by cluster, each cluster that has a concept is named at the middle of its points.
4. **Legend** of the current colouring (click entries to hide them), or for <UiLabel>Time</UiLabel> a note: <UiLabel>Older posts are pale, newer posts take the accent colour.</UiLabel>
5. **Summary**: how many posts, in how many clusters.

| <UiLabel>Colour by</UiLabel> | Colours |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Cluster</UiLabel> (default) | One colour per cluster; the legend names each cluster after its most common concept. Each concept names only one cluster (the one where it is strongest), so a cluster whose concepts all name other clusters is numbered instead: <UiLabel>Cluster 1</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Cluster 2</UiLabel>…. |
| <UiLabel>Author</UiLabel> | One colour per author, for the first eight authors. |
| <UiLabel>Kind</UiLabel> | Thoughts, replies, quotes, reposts and captured posts. |
| <UiLabel>Time</UiLabel> | A ramp from pale (oldest) to the accent colour (newest). |

## Explore

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Find a post's neighbours | Hover it. Lines join it to its five nearest posts in meaning, the other posts fade, and the tooltip shows its author, its opening words and, under <UiLabel>Nearest in meaning:</UiLabel>, the three closest with their similarity (1 is the same meaning). |
| Open a post | Click it. |
| Pan and zoom | Drag; scroll to zoom at the pointer. <UiLabel>Fit to view</UiLabel> resets. |

## How it is made

- **Placement.** Each post's embedding vector is projected onto its two main directions of variation (principal components). Nearness on the map is approximate: two directions cannot hold everything a vector says. The tooltip's neighbours are measured in the full space and are exact.
- **Clusters.** Posts are grouped by k-means in the full space, with about √(n/2) clusters, at least two and at most eight. Seeding is deterministic, so the same posts give the same clusters.
- **Neighbours.** Similarity is the cosine between two posts' vectors.

## Tips and limits

- Only posts embedded with the current model appear; after changing the embedding model, rebuild the index in Settings › <UiLabel>Embeddings</UiLabel>.
- Large networks are clustered on an evenly spread sample of 1,500 posts, and every post is then assigned to its nearest cluster.
