A time capsule is a note to the person you will be. Once sealed it is unreadable, even here, until its day comes. Then you open it, read what you wrote, and write back.

<Screenshot id="journal-capsules" alt="The Capsules tab: the Write to your future self button and a list with a ready capsule, two sealed capsules showing only their day and days left, and an opened one" caption="Ready capsules first, sealed ones by opening day, opened ones last." />

## Open it

Click the <UiLabel>Capsules</UiLabel> tab on the [Journal](/docs/guides/journal), or run <UiLabel>New time capsule</UiLabel> in the command palette to go straight to the form.

## Seal a capsule

<Screenshot id="journal-capsule-form" alt="The A note to your future self form: the note field, the opening day field with 30 typed and opens on a date below it, Add images, Seal and Cancel" caption="Write, choose the day, seal." size="md" />

<Steps>
<Step>
### Write the note
Click <UiLabel>Write to your future self</UiLabel>. Write in "Dear future me…". "It stays sealed, unreadable even here, until the day you choose."
</Step>
<Step>
### Add images (optional)
Click <UiLabel>Add images</UiLabel> and choose files. They are copied into your media folder; the form counts them ("2 images").
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose the day
In the opening field, type a number of days from today (`30`) or a date (`2027-01-31`). "opens on …" confirms the day as you type.
</Step>
<Step>
### Seal it
Click <UiLabel>Seal</UiLabel>. <UiLabel>Cancel</UiLabel> closes the form without sealing.
</Step>
</Steps>

| Message | Fix |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Write something (or add an image) first.</UiLabel> | A capsule needs a note or an image. |
| <UiLabel>Choose a day after today, as a number of days or a date like 2027-01-31.</UiLabel> | The day must be from 1 day to about 20 years (7,305 days) ahead. |

## While it is sealed

A sealed capsule shows only "From …" (the day you wrote it), "Sealed, N days left" and how many images it holds. Its note and images cannot be read anywhere in the app until the day comes.

## Open it on its day

From its day on (a journal day starts at 04:00), the capsule reads <UiLabel>Ready to open</UiLabel>. Simulacra announces it once with a notice, "A time capsule is ready to open in your Journal.", and the Today tab lists it with <UiLabel>Open</UiLabel>.

<Screenshot id="journal-capsule-open" alt="An opened capsule: From and Sealed for 1 day, the note and an image thumbnail, the Then and now prompt, the reply field, and Save reply, Turn into a post and Close" caption="Then and now: read what you wrote, and answer it." size="md" />

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open it
Click <UiLabel>Open</UiLabel>. The capsule shows when it was written, "Sealed for N days", the note and its images.
</Step>
<Step>
### Compare then and now
Under <UiLabel>Then and now</UiLabel>: "What has changed since you wrote this? What stayed the same? What would you tell the person who wrote it?"
</Step>
<Step>
### Reply
Write in "Your reply to your past self" and click <UiLabel>Save reply</UiLabel>. The reply is kept with the capsule.
</Step>
</Steps>

<UiLabel>Turn into a post</UiLabel> opens the composer with the note. <UiLabel>Close</UiLabel> returns to the list, where an opened capsule offers <UiLabel>Read</UiLabel> (a quiet button: only a ready capsule's <UiLabel>Open</UiLabel> is filled).

## Delete a capsule

Click <UiLabel>Delete</UiLabel> on its row. The confirmation warns: "The capsule and its letter are removed for good, sealed or not."

## Empty state

<UiLabel>No time capsules yet</UiLabel>: "Write a note to the person you will be. It stays sealed until its day." with <UiLabel>Write to your future self</UiLabel>.
