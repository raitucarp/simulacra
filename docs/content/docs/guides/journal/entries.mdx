Entries are free Markdown writing, one or more per day. A prompt or a template gets you started; the heat strip and your runs show how often you write, without ever scolding you for a day off.

<Screenshot id="journal-entries" alt="The Journal tab: New entry and template buttons, the Days written heat strip with three tiles, the search field and a list of entries" caption="Every entry, newest first, with the search and the heat strip." />

## Write an entry

<Steps>
<Step>
### Start it
Pick one:
- <UiLabel>Write about it</UiLabel> on the Today tab: a new entry answering today's prompt, which shows above the text.
- <UiLabel>New entry</UiLabel> on the Journal tab: a blank entry.
- A template button: <UiLabel>Morning page</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Evening review</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>Weekly review</UiLabel>.
- The palette command <UiLabel>New journal entry</UiLabel>.
</Step>
<Step>
### Write
The editor opens on the Journal tab with the text field focused: "Write freely. Markdown works; link a post or a vault document with `[[` and its id or title `]]`."
</Step>
<Step>
### Save
Click <UiLabel>Save</UiLabel>. The status beside the date changes from <UiLabel>Not saved yet</UiLabel> to <UiLabel>Saved</UiLabel>, and the editor stays open. <UiLabel>Close</UiLabel> saves and closes.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot id="journal-editor" alt="The entry editor on an Evening review: the date and Not saved yet status, the section headings in the text field, the links hint and the Save, Preview, Turn into a post and Close buttons" caption="An entry started from the Evening review template." />

An entry with nothing but spaces is not kept. Editing an entry keeps its day and creation time.

### Editor buttons

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Save</UiLabel> | Saves the entry; the editor stays on it |
| <UiLabel>Preview</UiLabel> / <UiLabel>Write</UiLabel> | Shows the text as it reads, with `[[links]]` as pills, or goes back to editing |
| <UiLabel>Turn into a post</UiLabel> | Opens the composer with the selected text, or the whole entry with no selection |
| <UiLabel>Close</UiLabel> | Saves, then closes the editor |

In the preview, a `[[post id]]` or `[[document title]]` pill opens that post or vault entry. The pill shows the post's opening words or the document's title.

<UiLabel>Turn into a post</UiLabel> copies the text into the composer on a new thread. Nothing is published until you post it there, as the persona you choose; the entry itself stays private.

## Prompts

Prompts come from research on reflective writing. Each kind has two wordings; every prompt comes once in a cycle of twelve days, in a shuffled order, and two days in a row never share one.

| Kind | Tradition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Expressive writing</UiLabel> | Writing about thoughts and feelings (Pennebaker) | "What has been weighing on you lately? Write freely for a few minutes without correcting anything." |
| <UiLabel>Gratitude</UiLabel> | Counting blessings (Emmons and McCullough) | "Name three things that went well today, and why each of them happened." |
| <UiLabel>If-then plan</UiLabel> | Implementation intentions (Gollwitzer) | "Which obstacle do you expect this week? Write: if it shows up, then I will…" |
| <UiLabel>What I learnt</UiLabel> | Noticing what was learnt | "Which idea changed your mind a little today?" |
| <UiLabel>What surprised me</UiLabel> | Noticing surprise | "What turned out differently from what you predicted?" |
| <UiLabel>Best possible self</UiLabel> | The best possible self (King) | "Write about your best possible self in one area: work, relationships or health." |

<UiLabel>Another prompt</UiLabel> on the Today tab shows the next prompt for the rest of the day.

## Templates

A template starts the entry with headed sections to fill in:

| Template | Sections |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Morning page</UiLabel> | Today's intention · Three priorities · I'm grateful for |
| <UiLabel>Evening review</UiLabel> | What went well · What I learnt · What surprised me · Tomorrow: if … then I will … |
| <UiLabel>Weekly review</UiLabel> | Highlights of the week · Lessons · What I'd change · Next week's intention |

## Find an entry

Type in <UiLabel>Search your journal</UiLabel>. Only entries holding every word you type are listed (any case, any order), with a short excerpt around the first match. The count reads "Entries: N"; "No entry has all of those words." when none match. An empty search lists every entry, newest first.

Each row shows the entry's first line as its title, the day and the word count, with <UiLabel>Edit</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Turn into a post</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>Delete</UiLabel>. Delete asks first: "The entry leaves your journal for good."

Entries can also be found from the command palette, under <UiLabel>Journal entries</UiLabel>, but only after you turn on **Settings › Privacy & Data › Include journal in search**. See [Privacy](/docs/guides/journal/privacy).

## Days written

The <UiLabel>Days written</UiLabel> strip shows one square per day, in week columns with Monday on top: 12 weeks in a compact window, 26 in wider ones. Hover a square for "day: N words". Beside it:

| Tile | Counts |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Days written</UiLabel> | Distinct days with an entry |
| <UiLabel>Current run</UiLabel> | Consecutive days ending today, or yesterday while today is still open |
| <UiLabel>Longest run</UiLabel> | Your longest run of consecutive days |

"Runs are just a record. Skipping a day is fine; come back whenever it helps." Today without an entry yet never breaks the current run.

## Empty state

With no entries the tab shows <UiLabel>Your journal is empty</UiLabel>: "A few minutes a day is enough. Start from today's prompt or a template." with <UiLabel>Write about it</UiLabel>.
