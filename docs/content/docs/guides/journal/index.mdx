The Journal is the one place in Simulacra that is only for you. Write a daily entry from a prompt or a template, check in on your mood and energy, run a focus session, or seal a note to open months from now. Nothing on this page reaches the stream, search by meaning, Insights, the agents or any AI.

<Screenshot id="journal-today" alt="The Journal page on its Today tab: the header with the privacy note and five tabs, a time capsule ready to open, today's prompt, the check-in card and the focus card" caption="Today: the day's prompt, a quick check-in and a focus start." />

## Open it

- Click <UiLabel>Journal</UiLabel> in the **left sidebar**.
- Press <Shortcut keys="Ctrl+8" />.
- Run one of the palette commands below.

| Palette command | Opens |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Journal</UiLabel> | The page as you left it |
| <UiLabel>New journal entry</UiLabel> | The editor on a new entry with today's prompt |
| <UiLabel>Log mood and energy</UiLabel> | The <UiLabel>Mood</UiLabel> tab |
| <UiLabel>Start a focus session</UiLabel> | The <UiLabel>Focus</UiLabel> tab |
| <UiLabel>New time capsule</UiLabel> | The capsule form |

## Anatomy

<Screenshot id="journal-header" alt="The Journal header: notebook art, the title, today's date with the current run, the lock with the privacy note, and the five tabs" caption="The header and its tabs." size="md" />

1. **Title and today.** Under <UiLabel>Journal</UiLabel>, today's date, followed by "N days in a row" while you have a run of written days.
2. **The privacy note**, with a lock: "Private: only you see this page. Entries, check-ins, focus sessions and capsules never appear in the stream, search by meaning, Insights or agents' context, and nothing is sent to any AI." See [Privacy](/docs/guides/journal/privacy).
3. **The tabs.** In a compact window they show icons only.

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Today</UiLabel> | Capsules ready to open, today's prompt, today's entries, the check-in and the focus card |
| <UiLabel>Journal</UiLabel> | Every entry, the search, the heat strip and your runs; the editor opens here. See [Entries](/docs/guides/journal/entries). |
| <UiLabel>Mood</UiLabel> | The check-in, charts and associations. See [Mood and energy](/docs/guides/journal/mood). |
| <UiLabel>Focus</UiLabel> | The focus timer and its log. See [Focus sessions](/docs/guides/journal/focus). |
| <UiLabel>Capsules</UiLabel> | Notes to your future self. See [Time capsules](/docs/guides/journal/capsules). |

## The Today tab

From top to bottom:

1. **Ready capsules**: one line per capsule whose day has come ("A time capsule from … is ready to open") with <UiLabel>Open</UiLabel>.
2. **<UiLabel>Today's prompt</UiLabel>**: the prompt's kind (for example <UiLabel>Gratitude</UiLabel>) and its text, with <UiLabel>Write about it</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Another prompt</UiLabel> and the three templates (<UiLabel>Morning page</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Evening review</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Weekly review</UiLabel>).
3. **<UiLabel>Written today</UiLabel>**: today's entries with their word count and <UiLabel>Continue</UiLabel>, once you have written one.
4. **<UiLabel>How are you feeling?</UiLabel>**: the same check-in as on the Mood tab.
5. **<UiLabel>Focus</UiLabel>**: "Pick one thing to finish, set a timer, and let the rest wait." with <UiLabel>Start a focus session</UiLabel>, or <UiLabel>Back to the running session</UiLabel> while one runs.

## Journal days

A journal day ends at 04:00 local time, like a review day: an entry written at 01:00 still belongs to the evening before. Each day gets one prompt, kept all day; <UiLabel>Another prompt</UiLabel> steps to the next one, and a new day starts fresh.
