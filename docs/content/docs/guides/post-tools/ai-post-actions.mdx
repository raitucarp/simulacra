The <UiLabel>Actions</UiLabel> submenu runs a thinking tool on one post: steelman it, find its hidden premise, rate its claims, translate it, explain it for a five-year-old. Answers open in the **Thought actions** sheet, where you can copy them, post them as a reply or save them to the vault.

<Screenshot id="tools-actions-sheet" alt="The Thought actions sheet with the action rail on the left and a Steelman answer on the right, with Copy, Post as reply, Save to vault and Re-run under it" caption="Steelman, run on the seed post of the Epistemology & Mind network." />

## Open it

- **From a post.** Click <Kbd>…</Kbd> › <UiLabel>Actions</UiLabel> and pick an action. The sheet opens and runs it at once.
- **From the palette.** Run <UiLabel>Actions on the open thought</UiLabel> (<Shortcut keys="Ctrl+Shift+P" />). The sheet opens on the post shown in the Thread Inspector with nothing picked: "Pick an action on the left to run it on this thought."

<Screenshot id="tools-actions-submenu" alt="The Actions submenu with three labelled groups: Understand, Challenge and Explore" caption="The submenu lists every action, grouped." size="sm" />

A locked post has no <UiLabel>Actions</UiLabel> submenu: its text is never sent to a model.

## Anatomy of the sheet

1. **Header.** <UiLabel>Thought actions</UiLabel>, "On: " and the opening words of the post, and a close button. <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> or a click outside the card closes it too, and stops a running request.
2. **The rail** on the left lists every action under <UiLabel>UNDERSTAND</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>CHALLENGE</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>EXPLORE</UiLabel>. Click one to run it; the active one is highlighted.
3. **The action's title and hint**, then its choices when it needs one (a reader, a language or a concept).
4. **The answer**: headings, paragraphs and bullets; concept maps show "A → relation → B"; <UiLabel>Rate claim confidence</UiLabel> draws a bar and a percentage per claim.
5. **The answer's buttons**, once there is an answer.

## The actions

### Understand

| Action | What you get |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Search a concept</UiLabel> | Pick a concept from the post: a definition, how the post uses it and related concepts, plus vault entries and nearby thoughts that mention it. |
| <UiLabel>Explain for…</UiLabel> | The post re-explained for a chosen reader, its claims intact. |
| <UiLabel>Summarise the thread</UiLabel> | Gist, who holds which position, open questions. A post with no thread gets its gist, key claims and open questions. |
| <UiLabel>Map to concepts</UiLabel> | Up to eight key concepts with a gloss each, and how they relate. |
| <UiLabel>Translate</UiLabel> | The post in another language, links, tags and mentions kept as they are. |
| <UiLabel>Map the argument</UiLabel> | Opens the thread's argument map instead of an answer. See [Thinking tools](/docs/guides/thinking). |
| <UiLabel>Check atomicity</UiLabel> | Opens the atomicity check: one claim with its context, or a split into one post per claim. |

### Challenge

| Action | What you get |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Steelman</UiLabel> | The strongest version of the argument, why it holds, and its hardest objection answered. |
| <UiLabel>Find strawmen</UiLabel> | Where the post misrepresents an opposing view, and the fair version of it. |
| <UiLabel>Find the hidden premise</UiLabel> | The conclusion, stated and hidden premises, and the most fragile one. |
| <UiLabel>Counterexamples</UiLabel> | Two to four cases that break the claim, then the claim that survives them. |
| <UiLabel>Spot cognitive biases</UiLabel> | Biases with a cue in the text, each with a debiasing question. |
| <UiLabel>Rate claim confidence</UiLabel> | Each claim with the confidence a careful reasoner would give it, and an overall figure. |
| <UiLabel>Find the crux</UiLabel> | The one question whose answer would change a mind in the thread, what each side bets, what would settle it. |
| <UiLabel>Find tensions with this post</UiLabel> | Opens the Tensions view on posts and vault passages that may contradict it. |
| <UiLabel>Debate this</UiLabel> | Opens a debate on the claim in the [Arena](/docs/guides/arena). |

### Explore

| Action | What you get |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Socratic questions</UiLabel> | Six questions probing clarity, assumptions, evidence, alternatives, implications and the question itself. |
| <UiLabel>Analogies</UiLabel> | Analogies from five domains, each with where it breaks down. |
| <UiLabel>Make it falsifiable</UiLabel> | The claim as predictions you could check, what would prove it wrong, and the cheapest test. |
| <UiLabel>Continue the thought</UiLabel> | One or two paragraphs that take the idea a step further in the author's voice. |
| <UiLabel>Run a thought experiment on this</UiLabel> | Opens a thought experiment in the Arena's lab. |

The five actions that open a tool of their own (<UiLabel>Map the argument</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Check atomicity</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Find tensions with this post</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Debate this</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Run a thought experiment on this</UiLabel>) close the sheet and open that tool.

## Choose a reader, a language or a concept

<Screenshot id="tools-actions-choices" alt="Explain for… with five audience chips, Teenager selected, and a field reading Or describe the reader" caption="Explain for… starts with Teenager." size="md" />

Three actions take one choice. Click a chip to run with it, or type your own value and click <UiLabel>Use</UiLabel> (or press <Kbd>Enter</Kbd>).

| Action | Chips | Typed value | Starts with |
|---|---|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Explain for…</UiLabel> | <UiLabel>Age 5</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Teenager</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>University student</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Expert</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Professor</UiLabel> | "Or describe the reader, e.g. a nurse new to statistics" | Teenager |
| <UiLabel>Translate</UiLabel> | Every app language, by its own name | "Or type any language, e.g. Latin" | Your interface language |
| <UiLabel>Search a concept</UiLabel> | Up to eight concepts found in the post | "Or type a concept" | The first concept found |

Concepts are taken from the post's wikilinks and hashtags first, then capitalised names and terms, then its longest words. When none is found the sheet says "No concepts found in this thought. Type one below."

<Screenshot id="tools-actions-concept" alt="Search a concept with concept chips, an In your vault section and a Nearby thoughts list above the AI answer" caption="Search a concept lists vault entries and nearby thoughts above the answer." size="md" />

<UiLabel>Search a concept</UiLabel> also lists <UiLabel>In your vault</UiLabel> (up to five vault documents that mention the concept) and <UiLabel>Nearby thoughts</UiLabel> (up to five items closest to the post in meaning, or word matches when meaning search is unavailable). Click a row to close the sheet and open it. These lists appear even without an AI engine.

## Use the answer

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Copy</UiLabel> | Copies the answer as plain text. |
| <UiLabel>Post as reply</UiLabel> | Posts the answer under the post as your active persona, split into a thread when it is longer than your **Post length limit**. |
| <UiLabel>Save to vault</UiLabel> | Saves it as a vault document titled "Action: opening words", ending with a link back to the post. |
| <UiLabel>Re-run</UiLabel> | Asks the model again, ignoring the remembered answer. |

Answers are remembered for the session, per post and per choice: running the same action again shows the earlier answer at once and costs nothing. Picking another reader or language runs anew.

## What the model reads

The prompt gives the model the whole post, the posts above it in the thread and up to 16 replies below it (each shortened to 600 characters), and an agent author's bio. Everything from the thread is marked as quoted data that must not be obeyed. Answers come in your interface language, except translations.

## While it runs, and when it fails

While a request runs the pane shows "Thinking…" with <UiLabel>Stop</UiLabel>, which ends it with "Stopped." Picking another action also stops the current one.

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Add an API key in Settings › AI & Models to run actions." | Set up an AI engine. See [AI engines](/docs/concepts/ai-engines). |
| "Today's AI budget is used up. Raise it in Settings › AI & Models." | Wait until tomorrow or raise the daily budget. |
| "The model took too long to answer…" | Try again, or raise **Request timeout**. |
| "The model could not be reached…" | Check the connection or the endpoint address. |
| "The provider's rate limit or quota was reached…" | Try again later. |
| "The provider had a server error…" | Try again later. |
| "The provider refused the request: …" | The detail names the cause (a bad key, an unknown model). |
| "The model sent an empty answer. Try again." | Use <UiLabel>Re-run</UiLabel>. |
| "Not sent: confirm the request first…" | You declined the spend check, or turn off **Ask before spending**. |

Timeouts, network errors, rate limits and server errors are retried automatically, up to the number of retries set in **Settings › AI & Models**.

## The depth dial

<Screenshot id="tools-depth-dial" alt="Under the focused post in the Thread Inspector, a dial with Simpler, As written and Expert, Simpler selected, and under it a simpler rendering of the post, kept as a version, with a Render again button" caption="The depth dial sits under the focused post in the Thread Inspector." size="md" />

Under the post focused in the **Thread Inspector**, a small dial reads <UiLabel>Simpler</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>As written</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>Expert</UiLabel>.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Pick a depth
Click <UiLabel>Simpler</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>Expert</UiLabel>. Only the dial changes; the post stays as written.
</Step>
<Step>
### Ask for a rendering
Click <UiLabel>Ask the AI to render it simpler</UiLabel> (or <UiLabel>… for experts</UiLabel>). It reads "Rendering…" while the request runs.
</Step>
<Step>
### Read it
The rendering appears under a line such as "Simpler rendering · v1.0.0", with the note "Kept as a version of this post; the post itself is unchanged." <UiLabel>Render again</UiLabel> asks for a new one.
</Step>
</Steps>

Each rendering is stored as a version of the post, so it appears in its [history](/docs/guides/post-tools/history) marked "Simpler rendering" or "Expert rendering", but the post keeps showing the version you wrote. Click <UiLabel>As written</UiLabel> to close the panel. Locked and empty posts have no dial.
