A **bookmark** keeps a post one click away. A **collection** goes further: it gathers posts and vault documents in your own order, each with a note on why it belongs. Both live on the **Saved** shelf of the Knowledge page.

<Screenshot id="tools-saved-view" alt="The Saved page with its title, a New collection name field and Create button, Learn this collection, shelf chips for Bookmarks and three collections, and a list of saved items" caption="The Saved shelf, on the Bookmarks chip." />

## Bookmark a post

- Click <Kbd>…</Kbd> › <UiLabel>Bookmark</UiLabel> on any post. The toast says "Bookmarked." and the item turns into <UiLabel>Remove bookmark</UiLabel>.
- Or, with the post open in the Thread Inspector, run <UiLabel>Bookmark this post</UiLabel> from the command palette (<Shortcut keys="Ctrl+Shift+P" />). It toggles the same way. Without a post open it says "Open a post in the inspector first."

## Add a post to a collection

<Screenshot id="tools-collection-submenu" alt="The Add to collection submenu listing three collections, one ticked, then New collection…" caption="A tick means the post is already in that collection." size="sm" />

Click <Kbd>…</Kbd> › <UiLabel>Organize</UiLabel> › <UiLabel>Add to collection</UiLabel>. The submenu lists the active network's collections by name:

- Click a collection to add the post at its end ("Added to Reading list."). A ticked collection already holds it; clicking it takes the post out.
- Click <UiLabel>New collection…</UiLabel> to create one. The Saved page opens with the name field focused; type a name and click <UiLabel>Create</UiLabel>, and the post is filed in the new collection.

Collections are the vault's own collections, so documents filed under one in the [Knowledge Vault](/docs/guides/vault) appear in it too.

## The Saved page

Open it with <UiLabel>Saved</UiLabel> in the switch at the top of the Knowledge page (<Shortcut keys="Ctrl+2" />), or with <UiLabel>Open collection…</UiLabel> in the command palette.

1. **Header.** <UiLabel>Saved</UiLabel>, "Bookmarks and collections: posts and documents together, in your order.", the <UiLabel>New collection name</UiLabel> field with <UiLabel>Create</UiLabel>, and <UiLabel>Learn this collection</UiLabel>.
2. **Shelf chips.** <UiLabel>Bookmarks</UiLabel> and every collection, each with its item count. Click one to show it.
3. **The count**, "ITEMS: 4", and in a collection the hint "Drag a row by its grip to reorder."
4. **Rows.** Each shows the item's kind, its title (the post's opening words or the document's title) and a byline (author and time, or document kind and creators), with <UiLabel>Open</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>Remove from this shelf</UiLabel> buttons.

| In a collection you can | How |
|---|---|
| Reorder | Drag a row by its grip onto another row. |
| Add a note | Click <UiLabel>Add a note…</UiLabel>, write why it belongs, click <UiLabel>Save note</UiLabel>. |
| Remove an item | Click the remove button. A document is unfiled from the collection; it stays in the vault. |

Bookmarks cannot be reordered and carry no notes. Removing a bookmark from the shelf is the same as <UiLabel>Remove bookmark</UiLabel>.

<UiLabel>Open</UiLabel> shows a post on the Timeline, or a document in the vault.

## Learn a shelf

<UiLabel>Learn this collection</UiLabel> turns the shelf you are looking at (bookmarks or a collection) into a connection map titled "Collection: name", opened in the Review page's map editor. It is disabled while the shelf is empty. See [Connection maps](/docs/guides/review/connection-maps).

## Empty shelves

- "No bookmarks yet": "Bookmark any post from its … menu to find it here."
- "This collection is empty": "Add posts from their … menu, or file documents under it in the vault."
