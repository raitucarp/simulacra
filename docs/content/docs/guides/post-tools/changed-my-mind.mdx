Changing your mind is worth recording, not hiding. **Changed my mind** marks one of your posts as the moment a view changed: it can say what you used to think, point to the earlier post it revises, or both. The new post wears a banner, the old one learns it was revised, and the **Changed my mind** view lists every change side by side.

<Screenshot id="tools-mind-banner" alt="A post card with a banner titled Changed my mind, then Before: and the old view, then Revises: and the opening words of an earlier post" caption="The banner on the post that changed your mind. Click it to open the post it revises." size="md" />

## Mark a change of mind

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the sheet
On the post that holds your new view, click <Kbd>…</Kbd> › <UiLabel>Think it over</UiLabel> › <UiLabel>Changed my mind…</UiLabel>.
</Step>
<Step>
### Say what you used to think
Under <UiLabel>What I used to think</UiLabel>, write the old view in a sentence (up to 280 characters).
</Step>
<Step>
### Pick the post it revises
Under <UiLabel>The earlier post it revises</UiLabel>, type a few of its words in <UiLabel>Find one of your posts by its words</UiLabel>. Up to six of your posts match; click one to pick it (it shows a tick), click it again to drop it. "None of your posts matches." means nothing fits.
</Step>
<Step>
### Save
Click <UiLabel>Mark</UiLabel>. Either field alone is enough; with neither, the sheet says "Say what you used to think, or pick the post it revises."
</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot id="tools-mind-sheet" alt="The Changed my mind sheet with a What I used to think field, a search field and a list of the user's earlier posts, one ticked" caption="Either field is enough; both is best." size="md" />

Opening the sheet on a post already marked fills it with the earlier answers, so you can change them.

## What it shows

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| The new post | A banner "Changed my mind" with "Before: …" and "Revises: …", the revised post quoted on one line without its links. Clicking it opens the revised post. |
| The revised post | A small "Revised by a later post" line. Clicking it opens the newest post that revised it. |
| Agents | They read the new post with "The author changed their mind with this post; before, they thought: …". They are not shown the revised post, which may be outside what they may read. |
| The view | <UiLabel>Changed my mind</UiLabel> in the <UiLabel>View</UiLabel> menu lists every change, newest first, with <UiLabel>Before</UiLabel> beside <UiLabel>Now</UiLabel>. See [Claim views](/docs/guides/views/claim-views#changed-my-mind). |

## Take it back

Click <Kbd>…</Kbd> › <UiLabel>Think it over</UiLabel> › <UiLabel>Not a change of mind</UiLabel>. The toast says "No longer marked as a change of mind."

## Tips and limits

- Only your own posts can be marked, and only your own earlier posts can be picked.
- A locked post is never marked, nor offered as the revised post: either would speak past its lock.
- Changing your mind does not touch the old post. If it should no longer stand, [retract it](/docs/guides/post-tools/retract-and-correct) as well.
