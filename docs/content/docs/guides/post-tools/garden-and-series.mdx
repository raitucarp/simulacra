The stream is for what is new. Two menu items give posts a longer life: the **garden** keeps notes worth returning to outside the stream and lets them mature, and a **series** numbers posts as parts of one project and tracks its progress.

## Plant a post in the garden

<Screenshot id="tools-garden-submenu" alt="The Garden: Seed row of the … menu's Organize submenu, open as a submenu showing Grow to Budding and Return to the stream" caption="A planted post's garden submenu." size="sm" />

On one of your own posts, click <Kbd>…</Kbd> › <UiLabel>Organize</UiLabel> › <UiLabel>Plant in the garden</UiLabel>. The post becomes a **Seed** note: it leaves the stream, together with the replies under it, and the toast says "Planted as a seed. It now lives in the Garden view of your profile, outside the stream." Search, threads, links and your profile still find it.

A garden note grows through three stages:

| Stage | Meant for |
|---|---|
| **Seed** | Just planted: a rough thought worth keeping |
| **Budding** | Being worked on: linked, rewritten, growing |
| **Evergreen** | Settled: a note that holds up and is returned to |

Once planted, the item under <UiLabel>Organize</UiLabel> reads <UiLabel>Garden: Seed</UiLabel> (or the current stage) and opens a submenu:

| Item | Does |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Grow to Budding</UiLabel> | Moves it to the next stage (not offered once evergreen). Toast: "Tended: now Budding". |
| <UiLabel>Back to Seed</UiLabel> | Moves it back a stage (not offered on a seed). |
| <UiLabel>Return to the stream</UiLabel> | Drops the garden note and puts the post back in the stream ("Back in the stream"). |

The card shows its stage under the author, with the stage's drawing: "Budding note". Nothing is copied: the note is the post, so its versions, symbols and lock stay as they are.

The <UiLabel>Garden</UiLabel> view of your profile (or of a thread) shows the notes in beds by stage. See [Garden and series views](/docs/guides/views/garden-and-series).

## Number posts as a series

<Screenshot id="tools-series-sheet" alt="The New series sheet with a Title field, an optional Parts planned field and a Start the series button" caption="Starting a series makes this post part 1." size="md" />

<Steps>
<Step>
### Start the series
On one of your own posts, click <Kbd>…</Kbd> › <UiLabel>Organize</UiLabel> › <UiLabel>Add to a series</UiLabel> › <UiLabel>New series…</UiLabel>.
</Step>
<Step>
### Name it
Give it a <UiLabel>Title</UiLabel> of up to 60 characters ("A hundred days of drawing"). Optionally, set <UiLabel>Parts planned</UiLabel> to a whole number from 1 to 1000 to follow its progress.
</Step>
<Step>
### Start it
Click <UiLabel>Start the series</UiLabel>. The post becomes part 1: "Added to “A hundred days of drawing” as part 1".
</Step>
<Step>
### Add later parts
On the next post, <UiLabel>Add to a series</UiLabel> lists your series, the one added to most recently first. Pick one and the post gets the next number.
</Step>
</Steps>

Each part shows "Title · part 12" (or "part 12 of 30" with a goal) under its author, with a thin progress line and <UiLabel>Previous</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>Next</UiLabel> buttons to the neighbouring parts.

To take a post out, click <Kbd>…</Kbd> › <UiLabel>Organize</UiLabel> › <UiLabel>Leave “title”</UiLabel>. The other parts keep their numbers.

| Message | Why |
|---|---|
| "Give the series a title first, in at most 60 characters." | The title is empty or too long. |
| "Parts planned is a whole number from 1 to 1000, or left empty." | The goal is not a valid number. |
| "The post could not be added to the series." | It could not be saved. |
