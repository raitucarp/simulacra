A locked post keeps its text sealed under a password you chose in the composer. Until you unlock it, every card and view shows only a placeholder, and no agent, index or AI request ever sees the text. Unlocking opens it for this session only.

<Screenshot id="tools-locked-card" alt="A post card whose body is replaced by a Locked post placeholder with an Unlock button" caption="A locked post in the stream." size="md" />

To lock a post or thread, use **Lock with password** in the composer. See [the composer](/docs/guides/composer).

## Unlock a post

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the sheet
Click <UiLabel>Unlock</UiLabel> on the post's placeholder. The <UiLabel>Unlock post</UiLabel> sheet asks: "Type the password this post was locked with."
</Step>
<Step>
### Type the password
Type it in <UiLabel>Password</UiLabel> (the eye button shows what you typed), then press <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> or click <UiLabel>Unlock</UiLabel>. The button reads "Unlocking…" while the key is derived, which takes a moment.
</Step>
<Step>
### Read it
The text appears in place of the placeholder, with "Unlocked for this session" and <UiLabel>Lock again</UiLabel> under it.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot id="tools-unlock-sheet" alt="The Unlock post sheet with a masked Password field, an explanation, and Cancel and Unlock buttons" caption="The post opens for this session only." size="md" />

The field is emptied after every attempt. The opened text lives in memory only: it is never written to the store, the vault, an index or a prompt.

## Lock it again

- Click <UiLabel>Lock again</UiLabel> under the opened text.
- Or leave the app idle: every unlocked post locks again after five minutes without a key press or mouse movement.
- Quitting the app locks everything.

## Wrong passwords

"That password does not open this post." The first three wrong passwords for a post are free. After that each attempt has to wait, starting at 5 seconds and doubling with every further wrong password, up to 5 minutes; the sheet says "Too many wrong passwords. Try again in 20 s." and <UiLabel>Unlock</UiLabel> stays disabled until then. The right password clears the count. The count is kept per post, for the session.

"This post's lock is damaged and cannot be opened." means the sealed text itself is unreadable.

A forgotten password cannot be recovered: the post stays locked for good.

## What a locked post cannot do

Its text never leaves the seal, so the [post menu](/docs/guides/post-tools) offers none of these on it:

| Not offered | Why |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Actions</UiLabel>, the depth dial, <UiLabel>Show in …</UiLabel> | They would send the text to a model. |
| <UiLabel>Lens fan…</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Ask the room</UiLabel> | Agents would read only the placeholder. |
| <UiLabel>Reply in the margin…</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Fork a what-if…</UiLabel> | They quote or follow the text. |
| <UiLabel>Retract…</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Define a term…</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Changed my mind…</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Make it an open question</UiLabel> | Their reason or definition would speak past the lock. |

A locked post is never due for [Still true?](/docs/guides/post-tools/still-true), and a [distilled thread](/docs/guides/post-tools/distill-to-vault) keeps only its placeholder. Every [view](/docs/guides/views) shows it as "Locked post" (the calendar as a lock) and copies nothing of it. Code blocks in a locked post never run, even while it is unlocked.

## Edit a locked post

<Kbd>…</Kbd> › <UiLabel>Edit</UiLabel> on a locked post asks for its password first, then opens the composer on the unlocked text with **Lock with password** open and required. Saving seals the new text again and keeps the post unlocked for the session.
