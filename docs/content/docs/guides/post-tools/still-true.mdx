A claim you wrote a month ago may no longer be what you think. **Still true?** brings your own posts back once they have stood a while and asks you to look again. Confirming one sends it away for longer each time, so the claims you keep reaffirming bother you less and less.

You wrote this on a date, and buttons Still true, Revise, Retract and Ask the AI" caption="The strip on a claim that is due. The seed post of Epistemology & Mind is due on a fresh profile." />

## Where it appears

- **On the card.** A post that is due shows a strip under its author: "Still true? You wrote this on 2026-09-30." with four buttons. It shows in the stream and in the Thread Inspector.
- **In the Still true? view.** The <UiLabel>Still true?</UiLabel> entry of a thread's or profile's <UiLabel>View</UiLabel> menu lists the posts due for a check, the one due longest first. See [Claim views](/docs/guides/views/claim-views#still-true).

The strip appears only on your own posts, and never on a locked one.

## When a post is due

The rule is the same for every post:

| After | The post comes back in |
|---|---|
| Writing it, or its last edit | 30 days |
| The first confirmation | 60 days |
| The second | 120 days |
| The third | 240 days |
| The fourth and every later one | 365 days |

An edit restarts the count: the new version comes back 30 days after it was saved, and earlier confirmations no longer count.

Only claims are checked. A post is never due when it is locked, a repost, empty, a feeling, an open question, a question you asked the room, retracted, or archived.

## Answer the strip

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Still true</UiLabel> | Confirms the claim. The toast says when it returns: "Confirmed. It will come back in 60 days." |
| <UiLabel>Revise</UiLabel> | Opens the composer on the post. Saving makes a new version and restarts the 30-day wait. |
| <UiLabel>Retract</UiLabel> | Opens the retraction sheet. See [Retract and correct](/docs/guides/post-tools/retract-and-correct). |
| <UiLabel>Ask the AI</UiLabel> | Asks your AI engine whether the claim still holds. |

## Ask the AI

<Steps>
<Step>
### Click Ask the AI
The strip reads "Asking the AI whether it still holds…" and the button hides until the answer is in. Only one claim is asked about at a time.
</Step>
<Step>
### Read the verdict
The answer appears under the strip as "AI: " followed by STILL TRUE, OUTDATED or UNSURE and one reason, naming what changed if anything did.
</Step>
<Step>
### Decide
The verdict does not change the post. Confirm, revise or retract it yourself.
</Step>
</Steps>

The model receives the claim and the date you wrote it, marked as quoted data, and answers in your interface language in a sentence or two. A failed request shows "No answer from the AI: " and the reason in a toast. The verdict is kept for the session only.

## Tips and limits

- The strip keeps asking until you answer it. If a claim is fine as it is, <UiLabel>Still true</UiLabel> is the quickest way to quiet it.
- A corrected post can still come due; a retracted one never does.
- Confirming is stored on the post, so the schedule survives restarts.
