Click an image on any post card and it opens in the **media viewer** (lightbox): the image as large as the window allows, its description, and the post's other images one key press away.

<Screenshot id="media-lightbox" alt="The media viewer showing one image fitted to the window, the close button top right, previous and next buttons at the edges and the caption 2 of 3 with the image description" caption="The second of three images, with its description." />

## Open it

Click any image in a post: a single image, a tile of a gallery, or an attached image file. The viewer opens on that image and takes the keyboard.

## Anatomy

| Part | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Image | Centre | Fitted to the window, keeping its shape, on the app background. |
| <UiLabel>Close</UiLabel> | Top right | Closes the viewer. |
| <UiLabel>Previous image</UiLabel> / <UiLabel>Next image</UiLabel> | Left and right edges | Step through the post's images. Shown only when the post has more than one. |
| Caption | Bottom | `2 of 3` when there are several images, and the image's description (alt text) when it has one. Hidden when there is neither. |

## Keys

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> | Close |
| <Kbd>←</Kbd> | Previous image |
| <Kbd>→</Kbd> | Next image |

Stepping **wraps around**: next from the last image goes to the first. A click anywhere outside the buttons also closes the viewer.

## Galleries on the card

How a post lays out its images before you open them:

- **One image** shows at its own shape; a tall image is sized by height.
- **Two to four images** form a two-column grid of tiles, cropped to fill.
- An image with a description wears an <UiLabel>ALT</UiLabel> badge in its corner.
- An image that cannot be loaded shows a placeholder and <UiLabel>This image could not be loaded.</UiLabel>

<Screenshot id="media-gallery" alt="A post with a three-image grid, one tile wearing the ALT badge" caption="A three-image gallery. The ALT badge marks a described image." size="md" />

Feed cards draw a cached thumbnail when one exists; the viewer always shows the original file.

## Write good descriptions

Add a description when you attach an image, in the <UiLabel>Description (alt text)</UiLabel> field under it in the composer ([Attachments › Images](/docs/guides/composer/attachments#images)). It is what the viewer shows under the image, and it tells anyone who cannot see the picture what it holds.

## Tips and limits

- The viewer shows the images of one post at a time, at most four.
- Videos, audio and other files do not open in the viewer: their cards have <UiLabel>Open</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>Play</UiLabel>, which use your system's apps.
- Link preview images open the linked page, not the viewer.
