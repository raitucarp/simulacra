A learning goal gathers the posts, sources, review items and concepts you want to master by a date. Its progress is measured from what you actually do (recall, writing, tutor dialogues and tasks), and a forecast says whether you are on track. The weekly review is a short ritual that looks back, looks ahead and turns the week's gaps into tasks of a goal.

## Open it

On the [Review](/docs/guides/review) page, click <UiLabel>Learn</UiLabel>, then <UiLabel>Goals</UiLabel>.

<Screenshot id="review-learn-goals" alt="The Learning goals list with two goal cards, each with its title, why, target date, forecast badge and a progress track with a time tick" caption="Each card shows progress against the time already spent." size="md" />

## The goals list

The card header holds <UiLabel>Weekly review</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>New goal</UiLabel>. With no goals it reads: "No goals yet. A goal gathers the posts, sources, review items and concepts you want to master by a date."

Each goal card shows its title and why, a forecast badge, a track filled to its progress with a tick at the share of its time already spent, and a line such as "Target 2027-03-01 (42 d left) · 12 items · 85% retention · 3 new posts · 1/4 tasks". Click a card to open the goal.

| Forecast | Means |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Reached</UiLabel> | Progress is 95% or more, or you marked it reached |
| <UiLabel>On track</UiLabel> | Progress is within ten points of the time spent |
| <UiLabel>At risk</UiLabel> | Progress lags the time spent by more than ten points |
| <UiLabel>Overdue</UiLabel> | The target date passed before the goal was reached |

## Create a goal

<Screenshot id="review-learn-goal-new" alt="The New goal form with What do you want to learn?, Why does it matter?, four horizon chips and the target date line" caption="A title, a reason and a horizon." size="md" />

<Steps>
<Step>
### Say what and why
Fill <UiLabel>What do you want to learn?</UiLabel> ("For example: understand the Ethics") and, optionally, <UiLabel>Why does it matter?</UiLabel>.
</Step>
<Step>
### Pick a horizon
Under <UiLabel>By when?</UiLabel>, choose <UiLabel>2 weeks</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>1 month</UiLabel> (the default), <UiLabel>3 months</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>6 months</UiLabel>. The line below shows the target date.
</Step>
<Step>
### Create it
Click <UiLabel>Create goal</UiLabel>. The goal opens. <UiLabel>Cancel</UiLabel> goes back to the list.
</Step>
</Steps>

A goal needs a title (<UiLabel>Give the goal a title.</UiLabel>) and a target after today (<UiLabel>The target date must be in the future.</UiLabel>). These show only after you click <UiLabel>Create goal</UiLabel> with something missing, not while you are still filling the form in.

## A goal's detail

<Screenshot id="review-learn-goal-detail" alt="A goal's detail: forecast and progress, six measured tiles in two rows of three, the tasks list with a field to add one, and the linked material with chips and pickers" caption="Measured progress, tasks and linked material." />

The head has <UiLabel>All goals</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Mark reached</UiLabel> (or <UiLabel>Reopen</UiLabel>), <UiLabel>Gap radar</UiLabel> (the radar narrowed to this goal) and the bin (<UiLabel>Delete this goal</UiLabel>, at once and without asking). Under the title, a line such as "Target 2027-03-01 (42 d left) · 40% done, 55% of the time spent".

### Measured progress

| Tile | Measures |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Review items</UiLabel> | Items that belong to the goal (see below) |
| <UiLabel>Retention</UiLabel> | Their mean chance of recall today |
| <UiLabel>New posts</UiLabel> | Your posts on the goal since it started |
| <UiLabel>Tutor dialogues</UiLabel> | Dialogues on the goal's material |
| <UiLabel>Best level</UiLabel> | The highest Bloom level those dialogues reached |
| <UiLabel>Progress</UiLabel> | The weighted score, coloured like the forecast |

Progress weighs memory 40% (how settled the items are: their mean stability against three weeks), tasks 30% (done out of all) and practice 30% (posts plus dialogues, six making it complete, and the best level reached). A part with nothing to measure, such as no tasks, is left out and the others share its weight.

An item belongs to the goal when you linked it, when it is anchored to a linked post, thread or document, or when it is tagged with or asks about one of the goal's concepts.

### Tasks

Type in <UiLabel>Add a task and press Enter</UiLabel>. Click a task to tick or untick it. "No tasks yet: add one, or let the weekly review suggest some."

### Linked material

The <UiLabel>Linked material</UiLabel> card shows a chip per link, such as "Post · Substance is one and infinite…", each removable. Add more:

| Section | How |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Concepts and posts</UiLabel> | Type a concept and press <Kbd>Enter</Kbd>, or click <UiLabel>Link a post…</UiLabel> and choose <UiLabel>Link</UiLabel> (the post) or <UiLabel>Whole thread</UiLabel> in the picker |
| <UiLabel>Vault documents</UiLabel> | Click a document chip (up to six are offered) |
| <UiLabel>Review items</UiLabel> | Search your items and click one to link it (up to eight rows show) |

## The weekly review

<Screenshot id="review-learn-weekly" alt="The Weekly review: the date range, step 1 with five tiles and what you recalled and wrote, and the start of step 2 with due counts and step 3 with weak concepts" caption="Look back, look ahead, find the gaps, choose what comes next." />

Open it with <UiLabel>Weekly review</UiLabel> on the goals list. It covers the last seven review days, today included, in four steps:

| Step | What it shows |
|---|---|
| 1. <UiLabel>What I learnt this week</UiLabel> | Tiles for <UiLabel>Reviews</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Recalled</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>New items</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Posts written</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>Tutor dialogues</UiLabel>; a few items recalled, posts you wrote and <UiLabel>Concepts you wrote about</UiLabel>. A week with neither reviews nor posts reads <UiLabel>A quiet week: no reviews and no posts yet.</UiLabel> |
| 2. <UiLabel>What is due</UiLabel> | "Due today: N. In the coming week: N." and <UiLabel>Start review (N)</UiLabel> |
| 3. <UiLabel>Where the gaps are</UiLabel> | The three weakest concepts ("free will: 62% gap") and <UiLabel>Open the gap radar</UiLabel> |
| 4. <UiLabel>Next actions</UiLabel> | Up to five suggested actions, all ticked |

The suggested actions are "Review the items due this week (N)", then one per weak concept chosen by its weakest dimension and varied so one kind does not repeat: "Talk through … with the tutor", "Write a post on …" or "Find a source on … for the vault".

<Steps>
<Step>
### Untick what you will not do
Click an action to untick or tick it.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose the goal
Under "Add the ticked actions as tasks of:", click a goal chip. The first open goal is chosen by default.
</Step>
<Step>
### Add them
Click <UiLabel>Add N tasks</UiLabel>. Each ticked action becomes a task of that goal, worded as shown.
</Step>
</Steps>

Without an open goal the step reads <UiLabel>Create a goal first to keep these actions as tasks.</UiLabel>

### The weekly reminder

Turn on **Settings › Learning › Weekly review reminder** and choose the **Weekly review day** and the hour in **Remind me at**. On that day, at or after that hour, a notification says "Time for your weekly review: open Review, then Learn, then Goals."
