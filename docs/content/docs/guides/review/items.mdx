The <UiLabel>Items</UiLabel> tab lists everything you are learning in the active network, so you can fix a question, pause an item for a while, or stop learning it.

<Screenshot id="review-items" alt="The Items tab: a count line and item rows, each with its prompt, kind, due date and tags, and edit, suspend and delete buttons; one suspended row is dimmed" caption="Next due first; suspended and retired items are dimmed and listed last." />

## Open it

Click the <UiLabel>Items</UiLabel> tab on the [Review](/docs/guides/review) page. The post picker and the map editor return here when you leave them.

## Anatomy

The caption reads "N items, next due first". Each row shows:

1. **The headline**: the item's prompt (two lines at most), or the map's title for a connection map.
2. **The meta line**: kind (<UiLabel>Question</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Answer</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>Connections</UiLabel>) · state · tags. The state is <UiLabel>New</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Due today</UiLabel>, "N days overdue", "In N days", <UiLabel>Suspended</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>Post deleted</UiLabel>.
3. **The buttons**, shown as icons with hover text:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Edit</UiLabel> | Opens the learn sheet on the item, or the map editor for a map. The schedule is kept. |
| <UiLabel>Suspend (leave out of reviews)</UiLabel> | Takes the item out of sessions and quizzes until you resume it. |
| <UiLabel>Resume reviews</UiLabel> | Puts a suspended item back. |
| <UiLabel>Stop learning</UiLabel> | Asks <UiLabel>Stop learning this?</UiLabel> first, then removes the item. |

The order is: active items (learnt ones by due date, then new ones), then suspended items, then retired ones. Up to 400 rows are listed.

## Stop learning an item

<Steps>
<Step>
### Click Stop learning
Click the bin icon on the item's row.
</Step>
<Step>
### Confirm
The dialog explains: "It leaves your reviews. The post or passage stays, and its past answers stay in the statistics." Click <UiLabel>Stop learning</UiLabel>.
</Step>
</Steps>

The post or passage itself is never touched. To learn it again, add a new question or answer; it starts as new.

## Retired items

When you delete a post, its item is retired: it reads <UiLabel>Post deleted</UiLabel>, is dimmed, and never comes back in a review. It stays listed, and its past answers stay in the statistics; remove it with <UiLabel>Stop learning</UiLabel> if you like. Retired items have no edit or suspend button.
