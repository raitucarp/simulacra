---
title: Memory palace
description: Place review items and posts at the landmarks of imagined rooms, then walk the route and recall each one.
icon: MapPin

A memory palace (the method of loci) is a route through rooms you can picture. At each landmark you leave something to remember, with a vivid image to see there. Walking the palace visits the places in order: you recall what is placed, reveal it and grade it, and a placed review item is reviewed like in a session.

## Open it

On the [Review](/docs/guides/review) page, click <UiLabel>Learn</UiLabel>, then <UiLabel>Palace</UiLabel>.

<Screenshot id="review-learn-palace-list" alt="A palace card with room thumbnails and Edit, Walk through and delete buttons, above the Memory palace card with a name field, six room chips and Build palace" caption="Build a palace, then edit or walk it." size="md" />

## Build a palace

<Steps>
<Step>
### Name it
Under <UiLabel>Name</UiLabel>, type something you know well ("For example: my childhood home").
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose the first room
Under <UiLabel>First room</UiLabel>, pick <UiLabel>Hall</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Library</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Garden</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Kitchen</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Study</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>Stairwell</UiLabel>.
</Step>
<Step>
### Build it
Click <UiLabel>Build palace</UiLabel> or press <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> in the name field. The editor opens.
</Step>
</Steps>

Each palace card shows its rooms as thumbnails, "N rooms · N of N places filled", and <UiLabel>Edit</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Walk through</UiLabel> and the bin (<UiLabel>Delete this palace</UiLabel>, at once and without asking).

## The editor

<Screenshot id="review-learn-palace-editor" alt="The palace editor: the Route card with rooms and their move and remove buttons, a room scene with five numbered places, and the panel of the selected place with a placed post, its image cue and buttons" caption="Click a numbered place to fill it." />

1. **The head**: <UiLabel>All palaces</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>Walk through</UiLabel>.
2. **<UiLabel>Route</UiLabel>**, beside the scene and as tall as it: the rooms in walking order ("1. Hall", with "2/5" filled), each with up, down and remove buttons. <UiLabel>Add a room</UiLabel> offers the six kinds; a new room goes at the end.
3. **The scene**: the selected room's picture with its five places as numbered markers, filled where something is placed and ringed when selected.
4. **The place panel**, across the full width below: "Place 2: Mirror", then either what to place or what is placed.

Every room kind has five landmarks, left to right:

| Room | Places |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Hall</UiLabel> | Door, Mirror, Chandelier, Bench, Clock |
| <UiLabel>Library</UiLabel> | Ladder, Shelf, Globe, Lamp, Armchair |
| <UiLabel>Garden</UiLabel> | Gate, Tree, Fountain, Bench, Birdhouse |
| <UiLabel>Kitchen</UiLabel> | Stove, Shelf, Table, Sink, Window |
| <UiLabel>Study</UiLabel> | Bookcase, Painting, Desk, Plant, Window |
| <UiLabel>Stairwell</UiLabel> | Bottom step, Banister, Portrait, Landing, Window |

### Fill a place

An empty place reads <UiLabel>Nothing here yet.</UiLabel> and offers:

- <UiLabel>Place a review item</UiLabel>: search your items and click one;
- <UiLabel>Place a post…</UiLabel>: opens the post picker; click <UiLabel>Place here</UiLabel> on a row.

Once filled, the panel says <UiLabel>Review item placed here</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>Post placed here</UiLabel> with its text, and offers:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Vivid image cue</UiLabel> | "Picture something striking here, then press Enter." Saved as "Image: …" |
| <UiLabel>Image ideas</UiLabel> | Opens image ideas for the cue; a drawn picture can be set as the cue picture with <UiLabel>Use as the cue picture</UiLabel> |
| <UiLabel>Remove the picture</UiLabel> | Takes the picture off the cue |
| <UiLabel>Empty this place</UiLabel> | Removes what is placed, and its cue |

Placing something new on a filled place replaces it and its cue.

## Walk through

<Screenshot id="review-learn-palace-walk" alt="A palace walk at a revealed stop: the room scene with only the current place marked, the place name, the image cue and picture, the placed item's faces and four grade buttons" caption="Recall what you left here, reveal it, grade it." />

<Steps>
<Step>
### Start
Click <UiLabel>Walk through</UiLabel> on a palace card or in the editor. Choose <UiLabel>Every place</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>Only what is due</UiLabel> (places whose item is due today or new).
</Step>
<Step>
### Recall
Each stop shows the room with only its current place marked, and "Hall · Mirror". "What did you leave here? Recall it before you look."
</Step>
<Step>
### Reveal
Click <UiLabel>Reveal</UiLabel> or press <Kbd>Space</Kbd>. The cue, its picture and what is placed appear.
</Step>
<Step>
### Grade
Press <Kbd>1</Kbd>–<Kbd>4</Kbd> or click a grade. The walk moves to the next place.
</Step>
</Steps>

The progress row reads "Place N of N" with <UiLabel>End walk</UiLabel>, which returns to the editor. At the end: "Walk finished: N% recalled." With nothing to visit: <UiLabel>Nothing to walk: place items at the places, or walk every place.</UiLabel>

A stop with a review item (an item placed directly, or a placed post that is being learnt) is reviewed with your grade and logged as a palace review. A forgotten stop is not repeated: the route stays the route.
