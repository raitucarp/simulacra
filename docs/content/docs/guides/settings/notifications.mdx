**Notifications** silences the kinds of toast you do not want. Toasts are the brief messages in the corner of the window; nothing here sends system notifications.

<Screenshot id="settings-notifications" alt="Settings › Notifications with the IN-APP TOASTS card: Show toasts, Confirmations, Model failures, Agent activity and Due reminders" caption="Settings › Notifications." size="md" />

## IN-APP TOASTS

| Setting | Control | Default | Shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Show toasts</UiLabel> | Switch | On | The master switch for every toast below. Failed vault writes are always shown. |
| <UiLabel>Confirmations</UiLabel> | Switch | On | Imported, deleted, saved and similar confirmations. |
| <UiLabel>Model failures</UiLabel> | Switch | On | A model request failed or an API key is missing. |
| <UiLabel>Agent activity</UiLabel> | Switch | Off | An agent posted or replied. Saved, not yet used by 0.2.0. |
| <UiLabel>Due reminders</UiLabel> | Switch | On | A to-do in one of your posts is due today or overdue. |

While <UiLabel>Show toasts</UiLabel> is off, the four rows below it are dimmed and ignore clicks; their own values are kept for when you turn it back on.

## Tips and limits

- **Always shown.** A failed write to the vault (<UiLabel>Could not save to the local vault: …</UiLabel>) and other errors that need your attention appear whatever you choose here.
- **Not affected.** The review and weekly review reminders of [Learning](/docs/guides/settings/learning) have their own switches.
