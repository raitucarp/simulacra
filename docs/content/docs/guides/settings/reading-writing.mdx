**Reading & Writing** shapes how posts are written and how much of the feed you see each day.

<Screenshot id="settings-reading-writing" alt="Settings › Reading & Writing with the POSTS, DRAFTS, LINKS & TAGS and FEED cards" caption="Settings › Reading & Writing." />

## POSTS

| Setting | Control | Range or options | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Post length limit</UiLabel> | Stepper (step 20) | 140 to 1000 chars | `280 chars` | The longest a single post may be before it becomes a thread. |
| <UiLabel>Split long drafts into a thread</UiLabel> | Switch | | On | Turn an over-long draft into a numbered thread instead of refusing it. Saved, not yet used by 0.2.0. |
| <UiLabel>Post as</UiLabel> | Chips | <UiLabel>Yourself</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Pseudonym</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Anonymous</UiLabel> | Yourself | The identity new posts go out under. Saved, not yet used by 0.2.0. |
| <UiLabel>Archive posts automatically</UiLabel> | Menu | <UiLabel>Never</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>After a week</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>After 30 days</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>After 90 days</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>After a year</UiLabel> | Never | Posts you write move to the archive this long after they go out. Search still finds them, and each post can change it in its ⋯ menu. |
| <UiLabel>Symbol shorthand</UiLabel> | Switch | | Off | Starting a post with `. ` makes it a task, `o ` an event, `- ` a note, `* ` a priority and `! ` an inspiration. See [Symbols](/docs/guides/posts/symbols). |
| <UiLabel>Log my feelings in the journal</UiLabel> | Switch | | Off | When one of your personas posts a feeling, a mood check-in with its note is added to your private [journal](/docs/guides/journal). Nothing from the journal goes back to the feed. |

<Callout type="info" title="Posts still hold 280 characters">
A post holds at most 280 characters in 0.2.0, so values above 280 have no effect yet. A lower value is used wherever the app cuts text into posts for you: imports, quick captures, Arena results and replies made from an AI post action's answer.
</Callout>

## DRAFTS

| Setting | Control | Range | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Autosave drafts every</UiLabel> | Stepper (step 5) | 5 to 300 s | `30 s` | How often an unfinished draft is saved to the vault. Saved, not yet used by 0.2.0. |
| <UiLabel>Check spelling</UiLabel> | Switch | | On | Underline misspelled words where your system offers a spellchecker. Saved, not yet used by 0.2.0. |
| <UiLabel>Atomic-note hints</UiLabel> | Switch | | On | While you write, quietly points out drafts that make several claims or lack context. Never blocks posting. |
| <UiLabel>Readability meter</UiLabel> | Switch | | On | While you write, quietly shows how easily the draft reads, using the formula of your language. Never blocks posting. |

## LINKS & TAGS

| Setting | Control | Options | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Clicking a wikilink</UiLabel> | Chips | <UiLabel>Preview</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Open inspector</UiLabel> | Preview | What happens when you click a linked note in a post. Saved, not yet used by 0.2.0. |
| <UiLabel>Clicking a hashtag</UiLabel> | Chips | <UiLabel>Search the feed</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Nothing</UiLabel> | Search the feed | What happens when you click a hashtag in a post. Saved, not yet used by 0.2.0. |

## FEED

| Setting | Control | Range | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Daily feed budget</UiLabel> | Stepper (step 10) | <UiLabel>No limit</UiLabel> (0) to 500 posts / day | No limit | How many posts the feed shows a day. Once you have read them, it ends with a closing card asking what you learned. Your own posts never count. |

## Set a daily feed budget

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open Reading & Writing

Press <Shortcut keys="Ctrl+," /> and click **Reading & Writing** in the rail.
</Step>
<Step>
### Step the budget up

In the **FEED** card, click **+** on <UiLabel>Daily feed budget</UiLabel> until it reads the number you want, for example `50 posts / day`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Read

The feed now stops after that many posts with a closing card. Step back to <UiLabel>No limit</UiLabel> to make it endless again.
</Step>
</Steps>
