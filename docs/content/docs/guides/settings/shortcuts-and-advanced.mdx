The last categories of the rail are short: **Shortcuts** opens the shortcut sheet, and **Advanced** holds diagnostics and the reset of every setting.

## Shortcuts › KEYMAP

| Row | Button | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Keyboard shortcuts</UiLabel> | <UiLabel>Open shortcut sheet</UiLabel> | Closes Settings and opens the shortcut sheet, which lists every shortcut. <Shortcut keys="Ctrl+/" /> opens it from anywhere. |

Shortcuts cannot be changed in 0.2.0. All of them are listed in [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/reference/keyboard-shortcuts).

## Advanced

### DIAGNOSTICS

| Setting | Control | Options | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Log level</UiLabel> | Menu | <UiLabel>Errors</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Warnings</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Info</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Debug</UiLabel> | Warnings | How much the app writes to its log. Saved, not yet used by 0.2.0. |
| <UiLabel>Developer options</UiLabel> | Switch | | Off | Extra diagnostics in the interface, such as render counters. Saved, not yet used by 0.2.0. |

### RESET

| Row | Button | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Reset all settings</UiLabel> | <UiLabel>Reset all</UiLabel> | Puts every setting in this window back to its default. Themes, keys and your data are kept. |

## Reset all settings

<Steps>
<Step>
### Click Reset all

In Settings › Advanced, click <UiLabel>Reset all</UiLabel>. The button turns red and reads <UiLabel>Click again to reset</UiLabel>.
</Step>
<Step>
### Click again

Click it a second time. A toast says <UiLabel>Every setting is back to its default.</UiLabel>
</Step>
</Steps>

Changing any setting, switching category or closing Settings in between cancels the waiting reset.

What it resets and what it keeps:

| Reset | Kept |
|---|---|
| Every row of General, Appearance (reading width, reduced motion), Reading & Writing, AI & Models (timeouts, retries, budget), Embeddings, Images, Agents & Simulation, Learning, Import, Privacy & Data, Notifications, Code and Advanced. Autonomous posting goes back to off. | The theme, its colours, font, text size, density and corners; the AI provider, key and model; credentials; downloaded icon sets; panel sizes; and everything in your vault. |

To put one setting back, use the arrow beside it instead (<UiLabel>Reset to default</UiLabel>). To reset the panel sizes, use <UiLabel>Reset layout</UiLabel> in [Typography and layout](/docs/guides/appearance/typography-and-layout).
