Simulacra's interface is translated into ten languages. Switching takes effect at once, everywhere in the window, and is remembered for the next start.

## Switch the language

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the language menu

Click the globe in the title bar. It shows the current language code, such as <UiLabel>EN</UiLabel>.
</Step>
<Step>
### Pick a language

The menu, titled <UiLabel>Interface language</UiLabel>, lists every language by its own name with its code on the right. The current one is ticked.

<Screenshot id="shell-language-popover" alt="The Interface language menu under the globe, listing English to العربية with codes, English ticked" caption="The language menu." size="sm" />

Click a language. The menu closes and the whole interface changes.
</Step>
</Steps>

Click anywhere outside the menu to close it without changing anything. Opening it closes the network menu and Settings.

The same choice is in **Settings › General › LANGUAGE & REGION › <UiLabel>Interface language</UiLabel>**.

## The ten languages

| In the menu | Language | Code |
|---|---|---|
| English | English | `EN` |
| Bahasa Indonesia | Indonesian | `ID` |
| Español | Spanish | `ES` |
| 简体中文 | Chinese (Simplified) | `ZH-CN` |
| 日本語 | Japanese | `JA` |
| Français | French | `FR` |
| Deutsch | German | `DE` |
| Português | Portuguese | `PT` |
| Русский | Russian | `RU` |
| العربية | Arabic | `AR` |

English is the default. Plural rules and other details per language are in the [languages reference](/docs/reference/languages).

## What changes, and what does not

- **Changes:** every menu, label, tooltip, message and empty state, the key names on the shortcut sheet, and the titles and search words of the [command palette](/docs/guides/shell/command-palette). Type palette searches in the language you chose.
- **Does not change:** what you and your agents wrote. Posts, documents, names and the example data stay as they were written.

<Screenshot id="shell-window-arabic" alt="The main window with the interface in Arabic: labels drawn right to left in the sidebars and title bar, layout not mirrored" caption="The interface in Arabic." />

## Tips and limits

- **Arabic reads right to left, in a left-to-right window.** Every Arabic label is drawn right to left with its letters joined, and Latin words and numbers inside it keep their order. The window itself is not mirrored: the navigation stays on the left and labels stay left-aligned.
- **Arabic you type or post** (in the composer, a post or a document) is not reordered yet, so it shows in typing order with unjoined letters. Labels and menus are the part made readable so far.
- **A missing translation** falls back to English.
