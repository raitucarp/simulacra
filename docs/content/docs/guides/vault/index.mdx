The Knowledge Vault is a library catalogue of the active network's documents. Each document keeps its full text and a catalogue record, so you can browse it like a library, read it in a calm reader, cite it, and let the network's agents draw on it.

<Screenshot id="vault-overview" alt="The Knowledge Vault showing two books on a shelf, with the facet column on the left" caption="The vault of the Epistemology & Mind network: Ethics and Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus on the shelf." />

## Open it

| Way in | Where |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Knowledge Vault</UiLabel> | The left sidebar. The number beside it counts the active network's documents. |
| <Shortcut keys="Ctrl+2" /> | Anywhere in the window. |
| <UiLabel>Go to Knowledge Vault</UiLabel> | The command palette (<Shortcut keys="Ctrl+K" />). |
| A document under <UiLabel>Sources</UiLabel> | The left sidebar lists every document of the network; a click opens its entry. |
| A document in the palette | Typing a title in the palette lists it under <UiLabel>Vault documents</UiLabel>. |
| A source chip, a `[[wikilink]]` or <UiLabel>Open in vault</UiLabel> | Posts that cite a document, and document nodes in the Mind Graph, open its entry. |

Every network has its own vault. Switching networks shows that network's documents and starts browsing afresh.

## The Library and Saved switch

A switch centred at the top of the centre column chooses between <UiLabel>Library</UiLabel> (the vault described here) and <UiLabel>Saved</UiLabel> (your bookmarks and collections as reading shelves, see [Data](/docs/guides/data)). Opening a document from anywhere in the app switches back to <UiLabel>Library</UiLabel>.

- The search field (<UiLabel>Search titles, creators, subjects and text…</UiLabel>) filters as you type. The cross clears it.
- <UiLabel>Sort: Date added</UiLabel> opens the sort menu. The label names the order in force.
- <UiLabel>Shelf</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>Table</UiLabel> switch between covers on shelves and a sortable table.

<UiLabel>Browse by</UiLabel> lists every way the documents are described, each value with the number of documents it would leave. See [Filter with facets](#filter-with-facets).

The shelf or the table, newest first until you search or sort. Clicking a cover or a row opens the document's entry.

## Search the vault

Type in the search field. Every word you type must appear somewhere in a document, and a word also matches longer words that begin with it, so `spino` finds Spinoza. Words shorter than two letters are ignored, and accents do not matter.

The search looks in titles and subtitles, creators, subjects, the abstract and the full text. A match in the title counts most, then creators, subjects, the abstract, and the text last. While text is in the field, <UiLabel>Relevance</UiLabel> becomes available as an order.

## Sort the results

<Screenshot id="vault-sort-menu" alt="The sort menu open, with Relevance, Title, Date added, Date published and Most linked, Date added checked" size="sm" />

| Order | What it does | Starts |
|---|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Relevance</UiLabel> | Best text match first. Without search text it falls back to the newest first. | Best first |
| <UiLabel>Title</UiLabel> | Alphabetical, ignoring a leading "The", "An" or "A". | A to Z |
| <UiLabel>Date added</UiLabel> | When the document entered the vault. This is the default. | Newest first |
| <UiLabel>Date published</UiLabel> | The year of the <UiLabel>Published</UiLabel> date. | Newest first |
| <UiLabel>Most linked</UiLabel> | How many posts link to the document. | Most first |

Choosing the order already in force reverses it. Ties are broken by title.

## Filter with facets

<Screenshot id="vault-facet-rail" alt="The Browse by column with Subjects, Collections, Type, Reading, Discipline, Form, Period, Place and Language groups" size="sm" />

The facet groups, top to bottom:

| Group | Values |
|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Subjects</UiLabel> | The subject vocabulary as a tree. A chevron opens the narrower terms. |
| <UiLabel>Collections</UiLabel> | Every collection of the network, even empty ones. |
| <UiLabel>Type</UiLabel> | Book, Article, Note, Web page, Transcript, Dataset, Media, Other. |
| <UiLabel>Reading</UiLabel> | Unread, Reading, Read. |
| <UiLabel>Discipline</UiLabel> | The ten main classes, such as Philosophy & psychology. |
| <UiLabel>Form</UiLabel> | Treatise, Essay, Dialogue, Aphorisms and the other forms. |
| <UiLabel>Period</UiLabel> | Antiquity, Middle Ages, Early modern, Modern, Contemporary. |
| <UiLabel>Place</UiLabel> | The places typed on the records. |
| <UiLabel>Language</UiLabel> | Language codes, shown in capitals (EN, LA). |

Click a value to choose it; click it again to drop it. The rules:

- Values of one group are alternatives: choosing Book and Article shows both.
- Different groups narrow each other: Book and Philosophy & psychology shows only philosophy books.
- A subject includes its narrower terms: choosing Philosophy also finds documents filed under Metaphysics.
- Each count says how many documents the value would leave, given your other choices and the search text. Values that would leave nothing are dimmed.
- A group with no values is hidden. Clicking a group's heading folds it.

### Active filters

<Screenshot id="vault-active-filters" alt="A row reading 1 of 2, a Treatise chip with a cross, and Clear filters" size="md" />

While anything narrows the results, a row above them shows "1 of 2" (shown of total), one chip per chosen value, and <UiLabel>Clear filters</UiLabel>. Clicking a chip removes that value. <UiLabel>Clear filters</UiLabel> keeps the search text.

## Shelf and table

The shelf shows each document's generated cover, title (two lines at most), first creator, and year and type. A thin green bar shows reading progress; a check and <UiLabel>Read</UiLabel> mark finished documents; unread documents carry no mark.

<Screenshot id="vault-table" alt="The vault as a table with Title, Creator, Type, Year, Added and Links columns" />

The table has one row per document:

| Column | Shows | Click the heading to sort by |
|---|---|---|
| <UiLabel>Title</UiLabel> | A small cover, the title and the subtitle | <UiLabel>Title</UiLabel> |
| <UiLabel>Creator</UiLabel> | The first author | (not sortable) |
| <UiLabel>Type</UiLabel> | The document type | (not sortable) |
| <UiLabel>Year</UiLabel> | The year published | <UiLabel>Date published</UiLabel> |
| <UiLabel>Added</UiLabel> | The day it was added | <UiLabel>Date added</UiLabel> |
| <UiLabel>Links</UiLabel> | Posts linking to it | <UiLabel>Most linked</UiLabel> |

An arrow beside a heading shows the column sorting the table and its direction.

## The screens of the vault

The vault has five screens. <UiLabel>Back</UiLabel> on each returns to the screen you came from, through up to 32 steps of history.

| Screen | Opens from | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| Browse | <UiLabel>Knowledge Vault</UiLabel> | This page |
| Entry | A cover, a row, a related entry, a source chip | [Documents](/docs/guides/vault/documents#the-entry-page) |
| Reader | <UiLabel>Read</UiLabel> on an entry, or a line under <UiLabel>Contents</UiLabel> | [Documents](/docs/guides/vault/documents#read-a-document) |
| Subject index | <UiLabel>Subject index</UiLabel> | [Catalogue](/docs/guides/vault/catalogue#subject-index) |
| Add or edit form | <UiLabel>Add document</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Edit</UiLabel> | [Documents](/docs/guides/vault/documents#add-a-document) |

## Narrow windows

<Screenshot id="vault-compact-drawer" alt="A narrow window with the facets as a drawer over a dimmed shelf" caption="On a narrow window the facets open as a drawer." />

When the window is narrow:

- The facet column becomes a drawer. <UiLabel>Filters</UiLabel> in the toolbar opens it and reads <UiLabel>Filters (2)</UiLabel> while two values are chosen. Click outside the drawer to close it.
- <UiLabel>Subject index</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Import file</UiLabel> and the sort button show icons only; hover for their names.
- The table keeps the Title, Year and Links columns.
- An entry's cover and catalogue record move below its text, and the form's paired fields stack.

## Tips and limits

- The vault belongs to the active network. Documents of other networks are not shown, searched or counted.
- With <UiLabel>Reduce motion</UiLabel> on (Settings › Appearance) the vault's screens appear without fading in.
- The search text, chosen facets and sort order reset when you switch networks; the shelf or table layout stays as you left it.
