The **calendar** puts posts on the days they were written. The **future log** looks the other way: everything scheduled or due from today on, month by month.

## Calendar

<Screenshot id="view-calendar" alt="The calendar view with a month title, previous and next buttons, weekday headings and day cells listing the opening words of posts" caption="A month of posts, from your first day of the week." size="md" />

Pick <UiLabel>Show as a calendar</UiLabel> from a <UiLabel>View</UiLabel> menu.

1. **Header.** The month and year ("Nov 2026") between <UiLabel>Previous month</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>Next month</UiLabel>. When you are not on the current month, <UiLabel>This month</UiLabel> brings you back.
2. **Weekdays.** The grid starts on the day set in **Settings › General › First day of the week**.
3. **Days.** Each cell shows its date and the opening words of up to three posts written that day, in the order written, then "+2 more". Today's date is in the accent colour. Days of the neighbouring months fill the first and last weeks, without their posts.

Click a post to open it. A locked post shows a lock instead of its words.

The calendar opens on the month of the newest post in view (or this month when there is none). The month you move to stays while you remain on that thread or profile.

## Future log

<Screenshot id="view-future" alt="The future log with month headings, each line a day number, a clock icon or a to-do state symbol, and its text" caption="Scheduled posts and dated to-dos, soonest first." size="md" />

Pick <UiLabel>Show the future log</UiLabel> from a <UiLabel>View</UiLabel> menu.

The log lists, from today on:

- **Scheduled posts**, on the day they are due to go out, marked with a clock.
- **To-dos with a due date** written in the posts, on their due day, marked with their state's symbol. Done and dropped to-dos are left out.

Months read soonest first; within a month lines go by day, posts before to-dos on the same day. Each line shows the day of the month and the text.

Click a line to open its post. On a profile, clicking a scheduled post opens it for editing. The future log is the only view that shows posts still waiting to go out; schedule one from the composer with <UiLabel>Schedule…</UiLabel>. See [the composer](/docs/guides/composer).

With nothing ahead it says "Nothing scheduled or due from today on."
