Four views turn the honesty tools of the [post menu](/docs/guides/post-tools) into lists: which claims are due for a second look, where you changed your mind, which questions are still open, and which terms you have defined. Each is offered in the Thread Inspector and on a profile's <UiLabel>Posts</UiLabel> tab.

## Still true?

<Screenshot id="view-still-true" alt="The Still true? view listing claims, each with its text on one line and Due since and a date" caption="Claims due for a check, the one due longest first." size="md" />

Pick <UiLabel>Still true?</UiLabel> from a <UiLabel>View</UiLabel> menu. Each row shows the claim on one line and "Due since 2026-10-30". Click one to open it, where its strip offers <UiLabel>Still true</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Revise</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Retract</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>Ask the AI</UiLabel>.

The view applies the [due rule](/docs/guides/post-tools/still-true#when-a-post-is-due) to every post it lists; the strip itself appears only on your own posts. Locked posts are never due, so never listed.

Empty: "Nothing to check: no claim here has waited long enough to be asked about again."

## Changed my mind

<Screenshot id="view-mind-changes" alt="The Changed my mind view: each row has a Before column with the old view and the revised post, beside a Now column with the new post" caption="What you used to think beside what you think now." size="md" />

Pick <UiLabel>Changed my mind</UiLabel> from a <UiLabel>View</UiLabel> menu. Every [change of mind](/docs/guides/post-tools/changed-my-mind) among the posts in view, newest first:

- **<UiLabel>Before</UiLabel>**: what you used to think, then the revised post quoted in a quieter colour on at most two lines ("Revises: …").
- **<UiLabel>Now</UiLabel>**: the post that changed your mind, on one line.

Click a row to open the new post. Locked posts are never listed, nor shown as revised.

Empty: "No change of mind here yet."

## Open questions

<Screenshot id="view-open-questions" alt="The Open questions view listing questions, each with Asked and a date" caption="Questions still open, the one asked longest ago first." size="md" />

Pick <UiLabel>Open questions</UiLabel> from a <UiLabel>View</UiLabel> menu. Every [open question](/docs/guides/post-tools/open-questions) among the posts in view, oldest first, each with "Asked 2026-11-02". Click one to open it. A locked one shows "Locked post".

For every open question of the network at once, run <UiLabel>Open questions</UiLabel> from the command palette.

Empty: "No question is open here."

## Glossary

<Screenshot id="view-glossary" alt="The Glossary view listing terms in bold, each with its definition under it" caption="Every term the posts in view define." size="md" />

Pick <UiLabel>Glossary</UiLabel> from a <UiLabel>View</UiLabel> menu. Every [term](/docs/guides/post-tools/glossary-and-terms) defined by the posts in view, alphabetically, each with its definition. Click a term to open the post that defines it. A locked post defines nothing, so it is never listed.

Empty: "No term is defined here yet."
