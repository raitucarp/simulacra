The **thought cadence** heatmap in the right sidebar shows how much the active network wrote on each day of the past year. Click a day to narrow the stream to that day's posts; a chip above the feed says which day you are looking at.

<Screenshot id="view-cadence-heatmap" alt="The THOUGHT CADENCE section of the right sidebar: a posts-per-year badge, the hint line, a grid of day cells in shades of one colour, and a Less to More legend" caption="The cadence heatmap, on the Mind & Cadence tab of the right sidebar." size="md" />

## Read the heatmap

Open the right sidebar (<Shortcut keys="Ctrl+Alt+B" />) on its <UiLabel>Mind & Cadence</UiLabel> tab. The <UiLabel>THOUGHT CADENCE</UiLabel> section holds:

- **The badge**: how many posts the network has in the year shown, "42 posts/yr".
- **The subtitle**: "Hover a day for details · click to filter the feed", or, once a day is picked, "3 posts on 2026-11-16" ("No posts on …" for an empty day; dates follow the Date format setting).
- **The grid**: one cell per day, a column per week, the most recent weeks that fit the sidebar's width (up to a year). Rows start on Sunday, with Mon, Wed and Fri labelled; month names run along the top. Hover a cell for a tooltip such as "3 posts on 2026-11-16".
- **The legend**, <UiLabel>Less</UiLabel> to <UiLabel>More</UiLabel>: five shades. Days without posts take the empty tone; the other four shades split the days that have posts into quarters, so the darkest shade marks your busiest days, whatever your pace.

Days are counted in your computer's local time.

## Filter the stream to one day

<Steps>
<Step>
### Click a day
Click a cell. Simulacra switches to the Timeline if you were elsewhere, and the stream shows only the posts written on that day. The day is highlighted in the grid.
</Step>
<Step>
### Read the chip
A chip above the stream reads "Showing 2026-11-16".
</Step>
<Step>
### Clear it
Click the <Kbd>×</Kbd> on the chip, or click the same day in the heatmap again. Clicking another day moves the filter there.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot id="view-day-filter-chip" alt="A rounded chip above the stream with a calendar icon, the text Showing and a date, and a small close button" caption="The chip while the stream is narrowed to one day." size="sm" />

## Tips and limits

- The filter narrows the stream only. The Thread Inspector still shows whole threads, including replies written on other days.
- The filter applies before the feed's tabs and search, so both work within the chosen day.
- Each change of filter resets the stream to its first page of posts.
