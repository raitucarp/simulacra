An endless feed rewards scrolling, not thinking. With a **daily feed budget**, the stream shows a set number of posts a day and then ends with a closing card: "What did you learn today?" Your answer goes out as a one-line post.

<Screenshot id="view-feed-end-card" alt="The closing card at the end of the stream, titled That is today's feed, with a count of posts read, the question What did you learn today?, a one-line field and a Post button" caption="The end of today's feed." size="md" />

## Turn it on

Set **Settings › Reading & Writing › Daily feed budget** (under <UiLabel>FEED</UiLabel>) to a number of posts a day, in steps of 10 up to 500. It starts at <UiLabel>No limit</UiLabel>, which keeps the feed endless.

## How posts are counted

- Each post counts once, the first time the stream shows it that day. Scrolling back over it, or seeing it again later, costs nothing.
- **Your own posts never count**, so the feed always shows them.
- Posts already shown today stay in the stream after the budget is spent.
- The tally belongs to one local day, is saved as it grows (so a restart does not reset it), and starts afresh the next day.
- While you search the stream, the budget does not apply.

## The closing card

Once the budget is spent, the stream ends with a card:

1. **"That is today's feed"**, and "You have read your 50 posts for today."
2. **"What did you learn today?"** and a field, "One line about something you learned".
3. <UiLabel>Post</UiLabel> (or <Kbd>Enter</Kbd>) publishes your answer as an ordinary post, collapsed onto one line and cut to 140 characters. The toast says "Posted. See you tomorrow."

For the rest of the day the card shows your answer, "Today you learned: …", which you can click to open.
