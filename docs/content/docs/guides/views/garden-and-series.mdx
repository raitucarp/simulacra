Planted notes leave the stream, and series parts are scattered through it. These two views bring each back together. To plant a note or start a series, see [Garden and series](/docs/guides/post-tools/garden-and-series).

## Garden

<Screenshot id="view-garden" alt="The Garden view with a Budding bed and a Seed bed, each note with its stage drawing, its text, and a line saying when it was planted and how many replies its thread has" caption="The Garden view of your profile." size="md" />

Pick <UiLabel>Garden</UiLabel> from a <UiLabel>View</UiLabel> menu. On your own profile it shows every note you planted; in the Thread Inspector, the notes in that thread.

- **Beds** by stage, the most mature first: **Evergreen**, **Budding**, **Seed**, each headed "Budding · 2". A stage with no notes has no bed.
- **Notes**, in each bed the most recently tended first (when its stage last changed, or when it was planted). Each shows its stage's drawing, its text on one line, "planted 2026-11-02", and "3 replies in its thread" when it has replies.

Click a note to open it. A locked note shows "Locked post".

Empty: "Nothing planted yet. Promote a post to the garden from its menu, and it grows here, outside the stream."

## Series

<Screenshot id="view-series" alt="The Series view with a series title and a parts count, a thin progress bar, and numbered parts #1 and #2 with their text" caption="Each series with its parts in order." size="md" />

Pick <UiLabel>Series</UiLabel> from a <UiLabel>View</UiLabel> menu.

- **Each series** among the posts in view, the one added to most recently first, headed by its title and either "12 of 30 parts" (when it has a goal) or "12 parts", over a thin progress bar.
- **Parts** in number order, "#12" then the part's text on one line.

Click a part to open it. A locked part keeps its number and shows "Locked post".

Empty: "No post here is part of a series yet. Add one from its menu with “Add to a series”."
