Two views strip the cards away. The **rapid log** reads like a bullet journal: one short line per post, grouped by day. **Raw text** shows exactly what was typed, marks and links included, with one button to copy it all.

## Rapid log

<Screenshot id="view-rapid-log" alt="The rapid log view with day headings and one bullet per post, some bullets drawn as task and note symbols" caption="A profile's posts as a rapid log." size="md" />

Pick <UiLabel>Show as a rapid log</UiLabel> from a <UiLabel>View</UiLabel> menu.

- **Days** read newest first, each under its weekday and date (in the date format of Settings › General); within a day, posts read in the order they were written.
- **Bullets.** A post with [symbols](/docs/guides/tasks) uses its main symbol as its bullet, with its signifiers in front of it. Any other post gets a plain "•".
- **Text.** Each post as one line of reading text: formatting marks, `[[post_…]]` links and embeds are left out, and each to-do shows only its words, so "[ ] finish the draft ^fri !!" reads "finish the draft". To-dos are separated by " · ". The raw view below shows everything as written.

Click a line to open its post. With no posts the view says "No posts to log yet."

## Raw text

<Screenshot id="view-raw" alt="The raw text view with a count of posts and a Copy all button above blocks of monospaced text, each under an author and time line" caption="Raw text shows the marks, links and tags as typed." size="md" />

Pick <UiLabel>Show as raw text</UiLabel> from a <UiLabel>View</UiLabel> menu.

- **The toolbar** counts the posts ("12 posts") and holds <UiLabel>Copy all</UiLabel>.
- **Each block** starts with "Author · 2026-11-23 09:30" in a fixed format, then the post's text exactly as written, line by line, in a monospaced font.

<UiLabel>Copy all</UiLabel> puts every block on the clipboard, each as its heading line and its text, with a blank line between posts. Use it to paste a thread into another app or a plain-text note.

With no posts the view says "No posts to show as text yet."

## Locked posts

Both views show a locked post as "Locked post". <UiLabel>Copy all</UiLabel> copies that line in its place, never the sealed text.
