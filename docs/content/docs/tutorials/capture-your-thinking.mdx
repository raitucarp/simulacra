In this tutorial you use the **Epistemology & Mind** network of a fresh install as your notebook for one day. By the end you will have caught a passing idea in one keystroke, written a thread, planned the day as a checklist, recorded a voice note, sketched a diagram, and scheduled a question for tomorrow morning.

You need about fifteen minutes. No AI engine is required, except for the optional transcription step.

<Screenshot id="composer-day-timeline" alt="The Timeline at the end of the tutorial: the scheduled-post toast, and a drawing, a voice note and a checklist at the top of the stream" caption="Where you will be at the end of the day." />

## Before you start

- Open the **Timeline** (<Shortcut keys="Ctrl+1" />) and make sure the active network is **Epistemology & Mind** (see [Networks](/docs/guides/networks)).
- You post as your primary persona, **Arya Wiratama** (`@architect`), unless you switch.

## Morning: catch an idea

You are reading and a thought arrives. Don't stop to file it.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open quick capture
Press <Shortcut keys="Ctrl+Shift+Space" />. A small box opens at the top of the window.
</Step>
<Step>
### Type and save
Type `Is a mirror's reflection a kind of understanding, or only a copy?` and press <Kbd>Enter</Kbd>. The toast says <UiLabel>Saved to your Inbox.</UiLabel> The box stays open and empty.
</Step>
<Step>
### Save a link too
Paste a web address, for example `https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/mental-representation/`, and press <Kbd>Enter</Kbd>. A lone address becomes a link post with a preview card.
</Step>
<Step>
### Close the box
Press <Kbd>Esc</Kbd>. The button in the box showed `Inbox (2)`: both notes are waiting.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot id="composer-capture-box" alt="The quick capture box at the top of the window with a typed note, Save, the hint line and Inbox (2)" caption="Quick capture keeps the thought without breaking your focus." size="md" />

## Mid-morning: write a thread

The mirror question deserves more than one post. Write it out as a short thread that answers Sokrates' question in the seed conversation.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Reply to Sokrates
In the stream, find the post by **Sokrates of Alopece** that begins *Tell me, my friend*. Click its speech-bubble button. The composer opens as `Reply to @sokrates`, with his post above your draft.
</Step>
<Step>
### Turn on free compose
Click <UiLabel>Free compose</UiLabel> (the list-tree button) in the footer. Now each line is one post.
</Step>
<Step>
### Write three lines
Type:

```text
A reflection carries the shape of the sun, not its warmth.
  Understanding needs a model that can be wrong, and a mirror cannot be wrong.
So the question is whether prediction can be wrong in the way a belief can. #Epistemology
```

The preview under the text says `3 posts` and shows the second line indented under the first.
</Step>
<Step>
### Post them
Click <UiLabel>Post all</UiLabel>. Your reply thread appears under Sokrates' post, and the Thread Inspector focuses its last post.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot id="composer-free-compose" alt="Free compose with indented lines and the live preview of the posts they make" caption="Free compose: one line per post, indentation for replies." />

## Noon: plan the afternoon

A checklist turns a post into a small plan, with due days that stay fixed to today.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the composer
Press <Shortcut keys="Ctrl+N" />.
</Step>
<Step>
### Check the syntax
Click <UiLabel>To-do syntax</UiLabel> (checklist button in the footer) to see the card of boxes and tokens. Click it again to close.
</Step>
<Step>
### Write the plan
Type:

```text
Afternoon reading plan
[ ] Reread Ethics, part II ^today !! &desk ~1h
  [ ] Note three definitions ^today
[ ] Reply to Spinoza's post on substance ^tomorrow
[ ] Water the plants ^^weekly &home
```

While you type `^tom`, the <UiLabel>Complete:</UiLabel> row offers `^tomorrow` with its date; click it to finish the word.
</Step>
<Step>
### Post it
Click <UiLabel>Post</UiLabel>. The post shows a checklist with chips for priority, due day, context, estimate and repeat.
</Step>
<Step>
### Tick a box
Click the box of `Reread Ethics, part II` first. It refuses: `1 sub-task is still open: finish or drop it first.` Tick `Note three definitions`, then the parent. Its progress chip now reads `1/1` in green.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot id="post-checklist" alt="A post showing a nested checklist with due, priority, context and estimate chips" caption="Your plan as a checklist." />

## Afternoon: say it instead

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the voice note sheet
Click <UiLabel>New</UiLabel> in the left sidebar and pick <UiLabel>New voice note</UiLabel>.
</Step>
<Step>
### Record
On Windows, click <UiLabel>Record</UiLabel>, say a sentence or two, and click <UiLabel>Stop</UiLabel>. On other systems, click <UiLabel>Choose an audio file…</UiLabel> and pick a short recording.
</Step>
<Step>
### Caption and post
Type `First thoughts on the definitions` in <UiLabel>Caption (optional)</UiLabel> and click <UiLabel>Post voice note</UiLabel>. The card has <UiLabel>Play</UiLabel> and the note's length.
</Step>
<Step>
### Transcribe it (optional)
With an AI engine that takes audio set up, choose ⋯ › <UiLabel>Think it over</UiLabel> › <UiLabel>Transcribe voice note</UiLabel> on the post. The transcript appears on the card.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot id="composer-voice-sheet" alt="The New voice note sheet with Record, Choose an audio file…, the caption field and Post voice note" caption="The voice note sheet: record, or choose a file." size="md" />

## Evening: sketch it

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the drawing sheet
Click <UiLabel>New</UiLabel> and pick <UiLabel>New drawing</UiLabel>.
</Step>
<Step>
### Draw
Pick the accent ink and width <UiLabel>4</UiLabel>. Draw a circle for *substance* with two arrows out of it for *thought* and *extension*. <UiLabel>Undo</UiLabel> takes back a bad stroke.
</Step>
<Step>
### Post it
Caption it `One substance, two attributes` and click <UiLabel>Post drawing</UiLabel>. The card shows the sketch and <UiLabel>Open SVG</UiLabel>.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Night: a question for tomorrow

You want to ask the network something first thing tomorrow, not now.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Write the question
Press <Shortcut keys="Ctrl+N" /> and type `Can a prediction be wrong in the way a belief can? [[post_1]]`. The `[[post_1]]` links your question to Arya's seed post on semiotics.
</Step>
<Step>
### Pick the moment
Click <UiLabel>Schedule…</UiLabel>, then <UiLabel>Tomorrow at 9:00</UiLabel>. The line under the field says `Goes out on …`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Schedule it
Click <UiLabel>Schedule</UiLabel>. The toast says it stays out of the feed until then.
</Step>
<Step>
### Check the queue
Press <Shortcut keys="Ctrl+Shift+P" />, type `scheduled` and run <UiLabel>Scheduled posts…</UiLabel>. Your question waits there, with <UiLabel>Edit</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Publish now</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>Cancel post</UiLabel>. Close the sheet with <Kbd>Esc</Kbd>.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot id="composer-schedule-row" alt="The Schedule picker open in the composer with the Publish at field, the three presets and the Goes out on line" caption="Scheduling the question for tomorrow morning." />

## Before bed: empty the Inbox

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the Inbox
Press <Shortcut keys="Ctrl+Shift+P" /> and run <UiLabel>Open Inbox</UiLabel>, or click the <UiLabel>Inbox</UiLabel> tab above the stream. Your two morning captures are there.
</Step>
<Step>
### Connect a capture
Click the mirror note's speech-bubble button and reply `Answered in my thread on Sokrates' question.` Now the capture is part of a conversation instead of a loose note.
</Step>
<Step>
### Go back to the stream
Click <UiLabel>For you</UiLabel> to see the whole day in the Timeline.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What you learned

- <Shortcut keys="Ctrl+Shift+Space" /> catches a thought into the Inbox without leaving what you were doing.
- Free compose turns an outline into a thread of replies in one go.
- To-dos live inside posts, with due days fixed to the day you wrote them.
- Voice notes and drawings are posts too, with optional captions.
- <UiLabel>Schedule…</UiLabel> holds a post back until its moment.
