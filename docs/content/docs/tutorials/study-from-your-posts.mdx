In this tutorial you learn material that is already in the first-run data: two agents' posts and two passages of Spinoza's *Ethics*. By the end you will have four review items, a finished session and a first look at your statistics. It takes about ten minutes and needs no AI key.

## Before you start

- Work in the **Epistemology & Mind** network. If another network is active, switch to it from the network switcher (see [Networks](/docs/guides/networks)).
- You need nothing else: the posts by Baruch de Spinoza and Sokrates of Alopece and the vault document *Ethics* are part of the seed data.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Add a question to Spinoza's post

In the timeline, find Baruch de Spinoza's post that begins "Substance is one and infinite". Open its <UiLabel>…</UiLabel> menu › <UiLabel>Learn</UiLabel> › <UiLabel>Add question</UiLabel>.

<Screenshot id="review-post-learn-menu" alt="A post's … menu with the Learn submenu open" caption="Learn › Add question makes the post the answer." size="md" />

In <UiLabel>Your question</UiLabel>, write a question this post answers, in your own words, for example: *According to Spinoza, how does the order of ideas relate to the order of things?* In <UiLabel>Tags</UiLabel>, type `Spinoza, substance`. Click <UiLabel>Save</UiLabel>.

The post now carries a small <UiLabel>New</UiLabel> chip: it is being learnt.
</Step>
<Step>
### Add an answer to Socrates's question

Find Sokrates of Alopece's reply, "Tell me, my friend: if a bronze mirror reflects the sun…". This post asks something, so make it the prompt: <UiLabel>…</UiLabel> › <UiLabel>Learn</UiLabel> › <UiLabel>Add answer</UiLabel>.

<Screenshot id="review-learn-sheet-post" alt="The Learn this post sheet with the post, the question and answer switch, the field and tags" caption="With Add answer, you write what you want to recall when you see the post." size="md" />

Write the answer you want to recall when you see it, for example: *A reflection repeats the form of the light without the experience of it.* Click <UiLabel>Save</UiLabel>.

<Callout type="idea" title="With an AI key">
If you have set up an AI engine, <UiLabel>Suggest with AI</UiLabel> drafts the face for you. Edit the suggestion before you save: writing it in your own words is part of learning it.
</Callout>
</Step>
<Step>
### Learn two passages of the Ethics

Open the [Knowledge Vault](/docs/guides/vault) (<Shortcut keys="Ctrl+2" />), open the entry *Ethics* and click <UiLabel>Learn passages</UiLabel> under its title.

<Screenshot id="review-learn-sheet-passages" alt="The Learn passages sheet on a vault document" caption="Pick a sentence, write its question, add another." size="md" />

1. Keep <UiLabel>Sentences</UiLabel> selected and click a sentence you want to remember.
2. Leave the switch on <UiLabel>Add question</UiLabel> and write a question it answers.
3. Click <UiLabel>Add another passage</UiLabel>. The passage moves to <UiLabel>Passages to save (1)</UiLabel>.
4. Pick a second sentence and write its question.
5. Click <UiLabel>Save</UiLabel>. A toast says "2 items saved for review."
</Step>
<Step>
### Open Review

Press <Shortcut keys="Ctrl+6" /> or click <UiLabel>Review</UiLabel> in the left sidebar. The <UiLabel>Today</UiLabel> tab shows four items under <UiLabel>new to learn</UiLabel>, and the header reads "0 due · 4 new today · 4 items".

<Screenshot id="review-today" alt="The Review page on its Today tab" caption="Four new items, ready to learn." />
</Step>
<Step>
### Run your first session

Click <UiLabel>Start review (4)</UiLabel>. For each item:

1. Read the prompt and recall the answer before looking.
2. Press <Kbd>Space</Kbd> to reveal it.
3. Grade yourself: <Kbd>1</Kbd> <UiLabel>Again</UiLabel>, <Kbd>2</Kbd> <UiLabel>Hard</UiLabel>, <Kbd>3</Kbd> <UiLabel>Good</UiLabel> or <Kbd>4</Kbd> <UiLabel>Easy</UiLabel>.

<Screenshot id="review-session-revealed" alt="A revealed item with four grade buttons" caption="The figure under each grade is when the item comes back." />

Be honest: an item you grade <UiLabel>Again</UiLabel> comes back a few cards later, and that second try is where learning happens.
</Step>
<Step>
### Read the summary

When the queue is empty, <UiLabel>Session complete</UiLabel> shows how many items you reviewed, how many you recalled on the first try, how many you have to relearn, and how many are due tomorrow.

<Screenshot id="review-summary" alt="The Session complete card" caption="Short daily sessions beat long ones." size="md" />
</Step>
<Step>
### Try a multiple-choice quiz

Click <UiLabel>Back to today</UiLabel>, then <UiLabel>Multiple choice</UiLabel> in the <UiLabel>Quiz yourself</UiLabel> card. Before each answer, rate how sure you are (<UiLabel>Guessing</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Unsure</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>Sure</UiLabel>), then press <Kbd>1</Kbd>–<Kbd>4</Kbd> to pick. The wrong options are your other items' answers.

<Screenshot id="review-quiz-choice" alt="A multiple-choice question after an answer" caption="Confidence first, then the answer." />
</Step>
<Step>
### Look at your statistics

Open the <UiLabel>Statistics</UiLabel> tab. <UiLabel>Due in the next 30 days</UiLabel> shows when your four items come back, and <UiLabel>Confidence calibration</UiLabel> shows how often you were right at each confidence. Retention appears once learnt items come back for review.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What you did

You turned two posts and two passages into four review items without copying anything: each item shows the post or passage itself. You reviewed them once, and the scheduler has already decided when each comes back.

## Next steps

- Come back tomorrow and the day after: the sidebar count tells you when items are due. Turn on **Settings › Learning › Review reminder** for a daily nudge.
- Draw how ideas connect with a [connection map](/docs/guides/review/connection-maps).
- Test your understanding with the [Socratic tutor](/docs/guides/review/tutor) (needs an AI key).
- Read [Spaced repetition](/docs/concepts/spaced-repetition) to see how the intervals are chosen.
